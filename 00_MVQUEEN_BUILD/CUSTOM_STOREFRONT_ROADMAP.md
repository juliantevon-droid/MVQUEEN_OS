# MVQueen Custom Storefront Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Experience foundation

- Custom global shell
- Custom header/navigation
- Mobile navigation
- Editorial spacing and typography
- Accessibility primitives
- Custom buttons, links, forms, focus states
- Global motion/reduced-motion system

## Phase 2 — Editorial homepage

- Hero/editorial opening
- Curated entry points
- Brand story
- Featured edit
- Quiet trust/context section
- Newsletter/community invitation
- Footer ecosystem

## Phase 3 — Discovery

- Collection architecture
- Curated collection headers
- Product grid/card system
- Search
- Filters
- Sorting
- Empty states
- Pagination/load-more behavior

## Phase 4 — Product experience

- Editorial product media
- Product title/price hierarchy
- Variant experience
- Purchase controls
- Product story
- Details/fit/material/usage accordions
- Recommendations as curation
- Breadcrumbs
- Product structured data

## Phase 5 — Cart and commerce

- Custom cart presentation
- Quantity controls
- Remove/update interactions
- Subtotal and checkout hierarchy
- Mobile cart UX
- Empty cart experience

## Phase 6 — Intelligence

- Catalog content engine
- SEO generation
- Alt text
- Collection merchandising
- Internal linking
- Search discovery signals
- Analytics/conversion instrumentation

## Phase 7 — Quality gate

- Mobile QA
- Desktop QA
- Accessibility QA
- Performance QA
- SEO/schema QA
- Catalog integrity QA
- Purchase-path QA
- Unpublished Shopify verification
- Explicit human-controlled publish step

## Rule

Do not optimize for maximum visual density or maximum promotional pressure. Optimize for clarity, taste, discovery, confidence, and a feeling of intentional selection.

# Brand Rules
## MVQUEEN_OS / 02_Brand_Identity

---

## Purpose

Non-negotiable brand governance rules. These rules exist to preserve MVQUEEN's identity, emotional atmosphere, and luxury positioning as the ecosystem scales.

Every creative output, product decision, and communication must pass these rules before deployment.

---

## The Non-Negotiables

### 1. Emotional Consistency
- **Rule:** Every touchpoint must feel emotionally intentional, not accidental.
- **Do:** Use the Brand Bible as the source of truth for every emotional decision.
- **Don't:** Chase trends that contradict the Brand Bible.
- **Red Flag:** If you're unsure whether something feels "on-brand," run it through the Doctrine System.

### 2. Luxury Without Exclusion
- **Rule:** MVQUEEN is accessible luxury, not gatekeeping luxury.
- **Do:** Make customers feel invited, elevated, and capable of luxury.
- **Don't:** Create content or messaging that feels exclusionary or judgmental.
- **Red Flag:** "Only for the elite" energy. Always wrong.

### 3. Femininity Must Be Intentional
- **Rule:** Femininity is never performative—it is precise, strategic, and emotional.
- **Do:** Use feminine language, soft aesthetics, and emotional depth.
- **Don't:** Use pink/glitter/flowers as a feminine shortcut without emotional backing.
- **Red Flag:** Femininity that feels like decoration instead of architecture.

### 4. Voice Cannot Be Faked
- **Rule:** Every word must be intentional and Brand-Bible-aligned.
- **Do:** Use the Tone_And_Voice system and CTA Library without modification.
- **Don't:** Write raw AI copy without voice review.
- **Red Flag:** Copy that feels generic, cold, or salesy.

### 5. Product Alignment
- **Rule:** Every product must fit the MVQUEEN aesthetic, quality level, and price tier.
- **Do:** Use the Product Philosophy from 04_Products.
- **Don't:** Add random products that don't fit the narrative.
- **Red Flag:** "It's on-trend" is not a sufficient reason to stock it.

### 6. Visual Consistency Across All Channels
- **Rule:** The brand must look, feel, and read the same everywhere.
- **Do:** Use the color_system.md and typography_system.md on every platform.
- **Don't:** Deviate from the visual standards for "platform flexibility."
- **Red Flag:** Brand looks different on Instagram vs Shopify vs email.

### 7. Content Quality Over Quantity
- **Rule:** One perfect piece of content beats ten mediocre pieces.
- **Do:** Spend time on post design, captions, and creative direction.
- **Don't:** Post mediocre content just to hit posting schedule.
- **Red Flag:** Generic captions, blurry photos, or AI copy without review.

### 8. Pricing Discipline
- **Rule:** Prices must reflect value and positioning, never race to the bottom.
- **Do:** Use the pricing framework from 04_Products.
- **Don't:** Discount heavily to compete with fast fashion.
- **Red Flag:** Price drops that feel desperate or unstrategic.

### 9. Customer Experience = Brand Experience
- **Rule:** Every interaction shapes how customers feel about MVQUEEN.
- **Do:** Ship fast, respond thoughtfully, package beautifully.
- **Don't:** Cut corners on packaging, shipping, or customer service.
- **Red Flag:** Negative reviews about the unboxing experience.

### 10. Data-Informed, Not Data-Driven
- **Rule:** Use analytics to validate brand decisions, not to contradict them.
- **Do:** Track metrics from 14_Data_And_Analytics.
- **Don't:** Abandon brand values because metrics say something "converts better."
- **Red Flag:** "The data said to do it" overriding brand judgment.

## Visual Brand Rules

### Logo Usage
- Minimum size: 80px width (digital), 0.5" width (print)
- Clear space: 20px minimum on all sides
- Never distort, rotate, or modify the logo
- Always use provided logo files (never recreate)
- Color options: Full color (preferred), grayscale, single color on white
- **Red Flag:** Logo pixelated, distorted, or on clashing backgrounds

### Color Palette
- Use only colors from 02_Brand_Identity/color_system.md
- Never mix brand colors with trendy colors outside the approved palette
- MVQUEEN Gold (#D4AF37) is the primary accent—use it intentionally
- Never desaturate brand colors
- **Red Flag:** Bright pink, neon, or colors outside the palette

### Typography
- Cormorant Garamond for all headlines
- Jost for all body copy and UI
- Never substitute fonts
- Maintain proper hierarchy from typography_system.md
- **Red Flag:** Comic Sans, generic web fonts, or misaligned hierarchy

### Photography & Imagery
- Use photography from 02_Brand_Identity/photography_direction.md
- AI-generated images must be reviewed and approved before use
- Photography must feel cinematic, soft, and emotionally immersive
- Never use harsh lighting, cluttered backgrounds, or exclusionary imagery
- **Red Flag:** Overprocessed, overly filtered, or emotionally cold photography

### Packaging
- Use approved colors: Soft Cream + Blush + Gold + Charcoal
- Include brand mark on every piece
- Tissue paper and filler must feel luxury
- Every element must serve brand experience
- **Red Flag:** Plain brown boxes, cheap filler, missing branding

## Voice & Messaging Rules

### Language Must Be...
- **Emotionally intelligent:** Connect to feelings, not just features
- **Precise:** Use specific, intentional language (no filler)
- **Warm:** Feel inviting, not cold or corporate
- **Feminine:** Without diminishing or stereotyping
- **Confident:** Assume the customer is sophisticated

### Language Must Never Be...
- Generic or salesy
- Patronizing or overly cute
- Trendy or dated
- Full of exclamation points
- Apologetic or defensive
- Written at a kindergarten reading level

### Forbidden Words / Phrases
- "Just" (minimizes)
- "Actually" (defensive)
- "Literally" (overused)
- "For all your [category] needs" (generic retail speak)
- "Shop till you drop" (dated, exclusionary)
- "Girl boss" / "slay" (performative femininity)
- "Limited time only!!!!" (feels desperate)

### Required Voice Elements
- Use CTAs from 10_AI_Systems/CTA_Library.md
- Every product description must use the formula from 06_Tone_And_Voice
- Headlines must have pattern interrupt or benefit clarity
- Closing copy must deliver transformation, not just transaction

### Red Flags
- Copy that sounds like ChatGPT
- Product descriptions that don't explain why someone needs it
- Marketing copy that contradicts the Brand Bible
- Customer communication that feels like a template

## Content & Creative Rules

### Social Media
- Content must align with content pillars (from 05_SEO_And_Content)
- Never post without a clear purpose or message
- Photography must pass the brand aesthetic test
- Captions must follow the caption template structure
- Engagement must feel authentic, not forced
- **Red Flag:** Off-brand content, inconsistent aesthetic, generic captions

### Blog & SEO
- All blog posts must deliver transformation + SEO value
- Never sacrifice readability for keywords
- Include MVQUEEN perspective—what makes this different?
- Use brand examples, not generic how-tos
- Every post must drive toward a brand value
- **Red Flag:** Generic listicles, keyword-stuffed copy, no MVQUEEN perspective

### Email
- All email must feel personal and intentional
- Never send generic promotional emails
- Use welcome sequences (24_Email_And_SMS) for all new subscribers
- Every email must have ONE clear purpose
- Unsubscribe must be easy (legal requirement)
- **Red Flag:** Spammy subject lines, irrelevant content, hard to unsubscribe

### Ads (Meta, TikTok, Pinterest)
- Use ad copy from 10_AI_Systems/Ad_Copy_Prompts.md
- Ad creative must use approved photography
- Targeting must feel respectful, not invasive
- ROAS must track back to brand perception (not just sales)
- Never use fear, scarcity, or FOMO manipulatively
- **Red Flag:** Desperate copy, negative audience targeting, low-quality creative

## Product & Merchandising Rules

### Product Quality
- Every product must meet the luxury standard defined in 04_Products
- Quality must be consistent across all SKUs
- Packaging must feel premium (no cheap plastic)
- Product descriptions must be accurate and complete
- **Red Flag:** Quality inconsistency, cheap materials, inaccurate descriptions

### Product Naming
- Use the naming framework from 04_Products/Product_Philosophy.md
- Names must evoke emotion and identity, not just function
- Never use generic names like "Basic T-Shirt"
- Names must be memorable and on-brand
- **Red Flag:** Boring names, inconsistent naming convention, generic terminology

### Product Photography
- Follow the brief in 02_Brand_Identity/photography_direction.md
- Every product must have multiple angles
- Lifestyle shots must show transformation, not just model wearing product
- Lighting must be soft and cinematic
- Color accuracy must be verified in product photos
- **Red Flag:** Flat lighting, cluttered composition, inaccurate colors

### Collections & Curation
- Collections must tell a story, not just group random products
- Each collection must have emotional and strategic purpose
- Collection names must align with brand voice
- Never create collections that feel forced or inauthentic
- **Red Flag:** Random collections, no clear narrative, weak curation

## Escalation & Exception Process

**When to break a brand rule:**
You can only break a brand rule if:
1. It serves a documented strategic purpose aligned with the Doctrine System
2. You have written approval from the brand guardian
3. You plan to test and measure the impact
4. You commit to restoring the rule if the experiment fails

**Process:**
1. Document the rule you plan to break
2. Explain why and for how long
3. Define success metrics
4. Get written approval
5. Execute the test
6. Report results
7. Restore or modify the rule based on data

**Red Flag for Exceptions:** "Everyone else is doing it" is not a valid reason.

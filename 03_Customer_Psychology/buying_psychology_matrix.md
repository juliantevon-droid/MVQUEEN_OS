# Customer Buying Psychology Matrix
## MVQUEEN_OS / 03_Customer_Psychology

---

## Purpose

Maps the emotional journey from awareness to advocacy, with specific psychological triggers at each stage.

---

## Stage 1: AWARENESS ("I didn't know this existed")

**Psychological Trigger:** Novelty + Recognition  
**Customer Mindset:** Curious, open, unaware of need

**MVQUEEN Approach:**
- Content: Educational, not salesy
- Messaging: "Something new for someone like you"
- Channel: Social proof, editorial, recommendations
- Goal: "I'm interested"

**Red Flags:** Aggressive sales talk, fake urgency, poor targeting

---

## Stage 2: CONSIDERATION ("Could this be for me?")

**Psychological Trigger:** Relevance + Trust  
**Customer Mindset:** Evaluating fit, checking credibility

**MVQUEEN Approach:**
- Content: Transformation stories, before/after results
- Messaging: "See how this works for someone like you"
- Channel: Reviews, testimonials, detailed product info
- Goal: "This could actually help me"

**Red Flags:** Vague promises, hidden costs, hard to find info

---

## Stage 3: DECISION ("Am I ready to commit?")

**Psychological Trigger:** Value confirmation + Removal of friction  
**Customer Mindset:** Ready to buy but needs reassurance

**MVQUEEN Approach:**
- Content: Guarantees, ease of purchase, support
- Messaging: "This is for you. We've got your back."
- Channel: Clear pricing, easy checkout, immediate support access
- Goal: "I'm buying"

**Red Flags:** Confusing checkout, unanswered questions, poor customer service

---

## Stage 4: PURCHASE ("I'm taking action")

**Psychological Trigger:** Anticipation + Delight  
**Customer Mindset:** Committed, expecting delivery

**MVQUEEN Approach:**
- Experience: Exceptional unboxing, fast shipping, quality assurance
- Messaging: "Your order is on the way" → anticipation communication
- Channel: Multiple touchpoints (email, SMS, tracking)
- Goal: "This arrived beautifully"

**Red Flags:** Delayed shipping, poor packaging, lack of communication

---

## Stage 5: EXPERIENCE ("Is this actually good?")

**Psychological Trigger:** Results confirmation + Community belonging  
**Customer Mindset:** Testing product, forming opinion

**MVQUEEN Approach:**
- Experience: Clear instructions, quick results, community access
- Messaging: "How are you using this?", "Join our community"
- Channel: How-to content, community group, customer stories
- Goal: "This actually works for me"

**Red Flags:** No follow-up, no community, no support for questions

---

## Stage 6: TRANSFORMATION ("This changed something for me")

**Psychological Trigger:** Identity shift + Pride in choice  
**Customer Mindset:** Emotionally invested, seeing results

**MVQUEEN Approach:**
- Experience: Celebrate milestones, ask for stories, deepen community
- Messaging: "You're already different," "Share your story"
- Channel: Customer spotlights, transformation stories, VIP access
- Goal: "I'm proud of this choice"

**Red Flags:** Ignoring customer stories, making it transactional, pushing too hard

---

## Stage 7: ADVOCACY ("Everyone needs to know about this")

**Psychological Trigger:** Meaning + Generosity  
**Customer Mindset:** Wants to share, feels part of brand

**MVQUEEN Approach:**
- Experience: Referral rewards, brand ambassador programs, community leadership
- Messaging: "Help someone like you find this"
- Channel: Social sharing tools, referral links, ambassador invitations
- Goal: "I'm bringing my friends"

**Red Flag:** Transactional only (no emotional element)

---

## Psychological Principles at Each Stage

| Stage | Principle | Apply This |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Novelty | Make it feel fresh, not desperate |
| Consideration | Social Proof | Show real people, real results |
| Decision | Scarcity (light touch) | "Limited stock," not aggressive |
| Purchase | Excitement | Packaging that delivers wow |
| Experience | Efficacy | Product delivers results quickly |
| Transformation | Identity | Help customer see herself differently |
| Advocacy | Meaning | Make sharing feel generous, not transactional |

---

## Red Lights (Psychological Damage)

**Never do these** — they break trust permanently:

1. **Bait & Switch** — Promising one thing, delivering another
2. **Fake Urgency** — "Only 3 left!" (when you have 1000)
3. **Hidden Costs** — Adding charges at checkout
4. **Inauthentic Stories** — Fake testimonials, fake before/afters
5. **Ghosting** — Ignoring customer after purchase
6. **Shame Marketing** — Making customers feel bad to drive sales
7. **Overpromising** — "This will change your life" (when it won't)

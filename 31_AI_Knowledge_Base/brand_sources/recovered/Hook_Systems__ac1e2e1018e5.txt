# 👑 MVQUEEN — Hook Systems
### Platform-Specific Hook Formulas

---

## What This File Is

The hook is the first second. The first line. The first frame.
If it doesn't stop her, nothing else matters.

This file contains proven hook structures for every platform MVQUEEN operates on — TikTok, Instagram Reels, Instagram captions, email subject lines, Pinterest, and SMS. Each platform has different physics — different scroll speeds, different attention patterns, different emotional triggers.

The hooks here are not generic. They are calibrated to MVQUEEN's voice, her customer's psychology, and the specific way attention moves on each platform.

---

## THE HOOK PRINCIPLE

Every MVQUEEN hook does one of four things:

**1. Creates a gap** — starts something the reader needs to finish
**2. Mirrors her** — shows her something she already thinks or feels
**3. Makes a bold claim** — says something specific enough to be surprising
**4. Opens a scene** — puts her inside a feeling before she decides whether she's interested

Generic hooks (👇 Watch this! / You NEED to know this!) do not belong in MVQUEEN content. The hook should sound like MVQUEEN, not like every other brand trying to go viral.

---

## PLATFORM 01 — TIKTOK / REELS HOOKS

On TikTok and Reels, the hook is the first **0–3 seconds of audio or on-screen text**. It must stop the thumb before the brain decides whether to engage.

**The physics:** Fast scroll. High stimulus competition. She's already seen 40 hooks today. The hook that wins is either unusually specific, emotionally resonant, or says something she's been privately thinking.

---

### Formula 01 — The Private Thought
*Say what she's already thinking but hasn't said out loud.*

**Structure:** "The [thing everyone experiences but nobody says]..."

**Examples:**
- *"The reason you keep going back to the same products isn't loyalty. It's this."*
- *"Getting dressed for yourself hits different when you know exactly why you do it."*
- *"Nobody talks about how much the packaging matters. But it does."*
- *"The ritual isn't about the product. It's about the 10 minutes you give to yourself."*
- *"You can feel it when a brand actually cares. And you can feel it when they don't."*

---

### Formula 02 — The Specific Claim
*Make a claim specific enough to feel credible and interesting.*

**Structure:** "This [specific thing] changed [specific outcome] for me."

**Examples:**
- *"This is the only fragrance I've worn for 3 years straight."*
- *"I switched to this one product and my whole morning routine felt different."*
- *"There are maybe 5 pieces I own that I'd repurchase immediately if I lost them. This is one."*
- *"My skin looked different in 4 days. I'm not being dramatic."*
- *"This is the dress I reach for when I want to feel like I've already handled everything."*

---

### Formula 03 — The Scene Open
*Drop her into a feeling before she decides to engage.*

**Structure:** [Sensory moment or emotional scene — no context needed]

**Examples:**
- *"Getting dressed for no one in particular, music on, and everything just... working."*
- *"The morning where your skincare routine actually feels like something you want to do."*
- *"The kind of fragrance where someone asks what you're wearing three hours later."*
- *"The outfit that makes you walk differently. You know the one."*
- *"That feeling when you open the package and the packaging alone is worth it."*

---

### Formula 04 — The Contrast Hook
*Set up an expectation and immediately flip it.*

**Structure:** "[Common assumption] — but actually [unexpected truth]."

**Examples:**
- *"Luxury isn't about the price. It's about this."*
- *"You don't need more products. You need better rituals."*
- *"The most expensive thing in my routine costs under $30."*
- *"Self-care isn't always a spa day. Sometimes it's just this."*
- *"I used to think 'affordable luxury' was just a marketing phrase. Then I understood what MVQUEEN was actually doing."*

---

### Formula 05 — The Direct Address
*Talk to her identity, not her problem.*

**Structure:** "For the woman who [identity statement]..."

**Examples:**
- *"For the woman who gets dressed like it's a decision she made on purpose."*
- *"For the woman who's been romanticizing her routine before it was a trend."*
- *"For the woman who doesn't need a reason to buy something beautiful."*
- *"For the woman who treats her skincare shelf like it matters. Because it does."*
- *"For the woman who's quietly building a world that feels exactly right."*

## PLATFORM 02 — INSTAGRAM CAPTION HOOKS

Instagram captions have more time than TikTok but the first line must still earn the "more" tap. The caption hook is the single line visible before the fold.

**The physics:** She's in a slower scroll. She's already seen the visual. The caption hook should deepen what the image started — not restate it.

---

### Formula 01 — The Continuation
*Continue the story the image started.*

**Examples:**
- *"She didn't get here overnight. But she gets dressed like she did."*
- *"The before isn't interesting. This is the after."*
- *"Some things you just know when you find them."*
- *"This is what 'treating yourself well' actually looks like in practice."*
- *"Not for the occasion. For the Tuesday."*

---

### Formula 02 — The Statement She'll Save
*Write something she'll screenshot.*

**Examples:**
- *"Getting dressed for yourself is one of the quietest forms of self-respect."*
- *"Luxury isn't about what you spend. It's about how it makes you feel when no one's watching."*
- *"She stopped waiting to feel like she deserved beautiful things."*
- *"Your routine is a reflection of what you think you're worth."*
- *"The version of you that moves through rooms like she owns them — she's already there."*

---

### Formula 03 — The Product Story Hook
*Open a product caption with the experience, not the product.*

**Examples:**
- *"There's a specific kind of morning where everything is exactly right. This is part of that morning."*
- *"Some purchases you forget. Some you reach for every single day. Different category entirely."*
- *"The ritual that starts before she gets to work."*
- *"It's the packaging. Then the scent. Then the way it makes her feel. In that order."*
- *"Not the product you needed. The one you didn't know was missing."*

## PLATFORM 03 — EMAIL SUBJECT LINE HOOKS

Email subject lines have one job: get opened. They compete in an inbox of 40+ messages. The MVQUEEN subject line wins by feeling personal, specific, or creating genuine intrigue — never panic.

**The physics:** She scans subject lines in under a second. She opens based on who sent it + first 5 words.

---

### Formula 01 — The Personal Tone
*Sounds like a message from someone who knows her.*

**Examples:**
- *"Something we made for you."*
- *"This one's yours."*
- *"We thought of you when we built this."*
- *"For the woman in your inbox right now."*
- *"You've been waiting for this, actually."*

---

### Formula 02 — The Specific New Thing
*Announces something worth opening for.*

**Examples:**
- *"New arrival: the collection you asked for."*
- *"Just dropped — and it's exactly what we knew it would be."*
- *"[Collection Name] is finally here."*
- *"The new ritual. Meet it."*
- *"Three new things. One of them is going to be yours."*

---

### Formula 03 — The Intrigue
*Creates a gap she needs to close.*

**Examples:**
- *"We don't usually do this."*
- *"The one piece everyone's been asking about."*
- *"Not the sale email. Something better."*
- *"She found it. Now it's your turn."*
- *"A note from the collection."*

---

### Formula 04 — The Occasion / Moment
*Ties the email to a feeling or time of day.*

**Examples:**
- *"For your Sunday ritual."*
- *"The Monday you deserve."*
- *"Tonight calls for this."*
- *"Because your morning should feel like this."*
- *"Before the weekend. This."*

---

### Formula 05 — Soft Urgency (Only when real)
*Use only when the scarcity or timing is genuine.*

**Examples:**
- *"Only 12 left."*
- *"Ends Sunday."*
- *"Last chance before it's gone for the season."*
- *"We restocked. It won't last."*
- *"Final week."*

## PLATFORM 04 — SMS HOOKS

SMS is intimate. It's the most direct channel — read within 3 minutes on average. Every SMS from MVQUEEN should feel like a message from someone who values her time.

**The physics:** One shot. No images. Pure words. She decides in 2 seconds whether this was worth receiving.

**Max length:** 160 characters for single SMS. Keep it under that.

---

### SMS Hook Examples

- *"Your new ritual just arrived. Shop the [Collection Name] → [link]"*
- *"We restocked the one you've been watching. It's yours if you want it → [link]"*
- *"New drop. This is the one. → [link]"*
- *"[First Name], we made something for you. → [link]"*
- *"The [Product] you asked about is back. Limited stock. → [link]"*
- *"Something new. Something worth it. → [link]"*
- *"The weekend collection is open. → [link]"*

## PLATFORM 05 — PINTEREST HOOKS

Pinterest is a saved-for-later platform. The hook here is in the pin title and first line of the description — written for search as much as desire.

**The physics:** She's building a mood board. She saves what inspires her world. The MVQUEEN Pinterest hook should feel like she's pinning something that belongs in the life she's building.

## HOOK TESTING PROTOCOL

Before publishing any hook, run it through these three checks:

**Check 01 — Does it sound like MVQUEEN?**
Read it aloud. Does it carry warmth, confidence, and femininity? Or does it sound like a generic content creator hook?

**Check 02 — Does it create a gap or mirror her?**
Would she read this and think *"yes, that's exactly it"* — or *"I need to know the end of that sentence"*? If neither, it's not working.

**Check 03 — Does it lead with feeling or product?**
If the first word is a product name or a brand mention, the hook is wrong. Lead with the feeling. The product earns its mention after the hook lands.

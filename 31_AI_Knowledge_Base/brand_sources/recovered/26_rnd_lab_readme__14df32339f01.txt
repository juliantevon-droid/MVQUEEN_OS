# 🧪 MVQUEEN — R&D Lab

---

## Purpose

The R&D Lab is MVQUEEN's experimentation and innovation layer — the protected space where new ideas, systems, products, and strategies are tested before they enter the main ecosystem.

Nothing in the R&D Lab is live. Everything here is in development.

**The rule:** Test here. Prove here. Then build into the main OS.

---

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Product Research | New product concepts, formulations, suppliers in evaluation |
| Content Experiments | New formats, styles, or platform strategies being tested |
| AI Experiments | New prompt approaches, chain architectures, agent concepts |

| Competitive Intelligence | Research on competitor brands, products, and strategies |
| Trend Research | Emerging trends being evaluated for brand alignment |

| Technology Research | New tools, platforms, or technologies being evaluated |

## R&D Process

```
IDEA → RESEARCH → SMALL TEST → EVALUATE → BUILD OR ARCHIVE
```

### Stage 1 — Idea Capture
All ideas are captured in `99_Command_Center/Brain_Dump.md` first.
Promising ideas are moved to the R&D Lab for development.

### Stage 2 — Research
Before any test, research the concept:
- Has this been done before? By whom?
- What are the risks?
- What would success look like?
- What resources does this require?

### Stage 3 — Small Test
Run the smallest possible version of the test first.
- New product: Sample order of 10-20 units before full inventory investment
- New content format: 3 posts before building a system around it
- New AI prompt: 10 outputs before adding to the prompt library
- New platform: 30 days of consistent testing before committing

### Stage 4 — Evaluate
Measure results against defined success criteria.
Document honestly — wins and failures both go in the log.

### Stage 5 — Build or Archive
- **Build:** Move to main OS, create proper system documentation
- **Archive:** Move to `98_Archive/Experimental_Systems/` with notes on why it didn't proceed

## R&D Lab Rules

1. Nothing tested here is live — it never goes to customers without graduating to the main OS
2. All experiments are documented — no undocumented tests
3. Failed experiments are archived, not deleted — they have intelligence value
4. R&D is protected from operational pressure — experimentation requires patience
5. Every idea starts small — never bet the business on an unproven concept
6. Doctrine still governs R&D — innovations must align with brand values

## What This File Is

A call-to-action is the moment the brand asks something of the customer.
In most brands, that moment sounds like a command.
In MVQUEEN, it sounds like an invitation.

Every CTA in this library was written to move without pushing. To invite without demanding. To feel like the natural next step — not a sales tactic.

Use this library across every platform, every format, every product category. Pull directly. Do not improvise generic CTAs — they break the voice.

## CTA RULES

### Rule 01 — One CTA Per Context
Never give the customer two CTAs competing in the same space. One action. One direction. One invitation.

> ❌ "Shop now · Explore collection · Sign up for updates · Follow us"
> ✅ "Shop the collection."

### Rule 02 — Never Use Generic CTAs
"Click here," "Learn more," "Buy now," "Submit" — these are placeholders, not brand copy. They do not exist in MVQUEEN content.

### Rule 03 — Arrows Are a Tool, Not a Decoration
The `→` arrow works in email and digital contexts as a directional cue. Use it only when it adds a sense of forward motion — not as decoration on every CTA.

### Rule 04 — Urgency Is Allowed — Panic Is Not
Soft urgency: *"Only a few remaining."* Hard panic: *"HURRY! DON'T MISS OUT!!!"*
MVQUEEN uses the first. Never the second.

### Rule 05 — Miss.Princess CTAs Never Appear in MVQUEEN Contexts
Her CTAs are more casual, more trend-aware. They belong in her world — not in MVQUEEN's.

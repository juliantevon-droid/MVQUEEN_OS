# 👑 MVQUEEN BRAND RULES
*The non-negotiable laws that govern every MVQUEEN expression*

---

## PURPOSE

Brand rules exist to protect the integrity of MVQUEEN across every touchpoint, every piece of content, every AI output, every campaign, and every team member's work.

These are not guidelines. They are laws.

---

## SECTION 1 — BRAND IDENTITY RULES

### R-01: The Name Is MVQUEEN — Always
Written as MVQUEEN — all caps, no space, no period.
✅ MVQUEEN | ❌ MV Queen | ❌ mvqueen | ❌ MV QUEEN

### R-02: The Sister Brand Is Miss.Princess — Always
Written as Miss.Princess — capital M, capital P, period between.
✅ Miss.Princess | ❌ Miss Princess | ❌ MISS PRINCESS

### R-03: The Full Meaning Is "Most Valuable Queen"
Always written out in full. Never abbreviated as MVQ.

### R-04: The Crown Is The Only Brand Icon
👑 is MVQUEEN's signature symbol. One per communication piece maximum in external content.

### R-05: The Two Brands Are Always Distinct
- MVQUEEN = mature, confident, elegant, elevated femininity
- Miss.Princess = youthful, playful, dreamy, soft glam
Never merged or confused.

---

## SECTION 2 — VISUAL IDENTITY RULES

### R-06: Always Use The Approved Color Palette
Primary: QUEEN NOIR · CHAMPAGNE IVORY · BLUSH REIGN · CHAMPAGNE GOLD · VELVET ROSE
Secondary: WARM NUDE · SOFT MAUVE · DEEP PLUM · ROSE SMOKE · BRONZE LIGHT
❌ No bright whites | ❌ No cool tones | ❌ No neons | ❌ No silver as primary

### R-07: Always Use The Approved Font System
Display: Cormorant Garamond (or Playfair Display)
Body: Jost
Fallback: Georgia (serif), Arial (sans-serif)
❌ No Roboto | ❌ No Open Sans | ❌ Max 3 fonts per layout

### R-08: Photography Must Always Feel Warm, Soft, and Elevated
Warm, soft lighting · Approved color palette backgrounds · Aspirational but accessible · Product in emotional context

### R-09: Packaging Is A Brand Experience, Not Just A Container
Base: CHAMPAGNE IVORY or QUEEN NOIR · Foil: CHAMPAGNE GOLD · Tissue: ROSE SMOKE · Ribbon: BLUSH REIGN or CHAMPAGNE GOLD · Personal brand-voice note inside

---

## SECTION 3 — VOICE AND LANGUAGE RULES

### R-10: Always Write In The MVQUEEN Voice
Warm · elegant · confident · emotionally intelligent · feminine
Refer to Writing_Rules.md and Voice_Consistency_Rules.md

### R-11: She Is Always "She" — Never Generic
Never: "the customer" | "users" | "buyers" | "people" | "consumers"

### R-12: The Brand Speaks To Her, Not About Her
✅ "You deserve this." | ✅ "Made for women who know their worth."
❌ "Women will love this product." | ❌ "Our customers enjoy..."

### R-13: Forbidden Words Are Absolute
Refer to Forbidden_Words.md — no exceptions.

### R-14: "Emotional" Is A Precise Tool, Not A Filler Word
Used once or twice max. Never as a default descriptor.
- Instead of "emotional elevation" → "she feels more herself"
- Instead of "emotional connection" → "she feels seen"
- Instead of "emotionally empowered" → "she walks differently"

### R-15: The Brand Never Apologizes For Its Prices
✅ "Luxury that doesn't ask you to choose." | ✅ "Elevated quality. Real-life price."
❌ "Affordable!" | ❌ "Low price!" | ❌ "Budget luxury"

---

## SECTION 4 — CONTENT RULES

### R-16: Every Piece Of Content Must Serve One Of Three Purposes
1. Inspire — make her feel something
2. Educate — teach her something
3. Convert — move her toward action

### R-17: Content Must Connect To At Least One Brand Pillar
Confidence · Femininity · Elegance · Softness · Luxury · Self-worth · Transformation

### R-18: No Trend-Chasing That Dilutes Brand Identity
Participate in trends only when they align with the brand's aesthetic, tone, and long-term identity.

### R-19: AI-Generated Content Must Pass The Brand Filter
All AI output reviewed against: Voice Consistency Rules · Forbidden Words · Brand visual alignment · Emotional register
AI is a tool. The brand is the filter. Human review is always final.

---

## SECTION 5 — POSITIONING RULES

### R-20: MVQUEEN Is Accessible Luxury — Not Fast Fashion
Everything must signal premium quality and intentional design — regardless of price point.

### R-21: The Brand Competes On Feeling — Not Price
She chooses MVQUEEN for how it makes her feel. Price comparisons and discount messaging undermine the brand.

### R-22: MVQUEEN Is For Women — Fully, Unapologetically
Every decision filtered through:
- How does this make her feel?
- Does this honor her?
- Does this elevate her?

---

## SECTION 6 — AI AGENT AND OS RULES

### R-23: Every AI Session Must Load Brand Context First
MVQUEEN_CONTEXT.md is the minimum requirement before any AI produces content.

### R-24: The Brand Bible Is Always The Source Of Truth
Conflicts between files → Brand Bible wins. Brand Bible vs. rebuilt file → human review before publishing.

### R-25: The OS Is A Living System — Files Are Versioned, Not Deleted
Outdated files archived in 98_Archive/Deprecated_Systems before replacement. Nothing deleted without human confirmation.

## THE 10 BRAND COMMANDMENTS

1. The name is MVQUEEN. Always.
2. The sister brand is Miss.Princess. Always.
3. The color palette is warm, feminine, and luxury. Always.
4. The typography is editorial serif + clean sans-serif. Always.
5. The voice is warm, confident, and emotionally intelligent. Always.
6. She is always "she." The brand always speaks to her, not about her.
7. "Emotional" is used with precision, not as a filler word.
8. Every piece of content inspires, educates, or converts.
9. The brand never competes on price — only on feeling.
10. AI is a tool. The brand is the filter. Human review is always final.

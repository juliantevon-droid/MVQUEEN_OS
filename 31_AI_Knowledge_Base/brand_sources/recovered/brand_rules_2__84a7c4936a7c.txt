# 👑 MVQUEEN — Brand Rules
### The DO / DON'T Reference

---

## What This File Is

Every brand decision — visual, verbal, experiential — produces either something that builds MVQUEEN or something that quietly erodes it.

This file makes those decisions fast and unambiguous. These are not suggestions. They are the guardrails that keep the brand coherent at scale — across designers, copywriters, AI systems, developers, and collaborators who may have read the Brand Bible once and need a quick reference.

When in doubt, this file answers.

---

## SECTION 1 — VOICE & COPY RULES

### ✅ DO — Voice

**DO lead with feeling before function.**
The product's emotional impact comes before its technical description. A woman should feel something in the first sentence before she learns anything.

> ✅ "The kind of confidence you don't have to announce. Our [product] works quietly — and so do you."
> ❌ "Our hydrating serum contains 3 key ingredients for moisture retention."

---

**DO write in short, weighted sentences.**
Three-word sentences hit harder than paragraphs when placed correctly. MVQUEEN copy earns its length — every sentence does work.

> ✅ "She already knew. This just made it visible."
> ❌ "This product is designed to help women feel more confident and beautiful in their everyday lives."

---

**DO use specificity over adjectives.**
Describe what something feels, looks, smells, weighs, sounds like. A list of adjectives (luxurious, beautiful, elevated) is not copy. It is a placeholder.

> ✅ "Matte finish. Weighted lid. The kind of packaging that makes throwing it away feel like a waste."
> ❌ "Beautiful, luxurious packaging you'll love."

---

**DO write to her directly — "she" in third person, "you" in second person.**
Pick one perspective and stay in it per piece of copy. Both work. Mixing them within the same piece does not.

> ✅ "She moves through the room differently when she's wearing this." (third)
> ✅ "You don't need a reason to feel this good." (second)
> ❌ "She deserves to feel her best, and you can help her do that." (mixed — confusing)

---

**DO end on identity, not product.**
The last impression in any piece of copy connects to who she is — not what we're selling.

> ✅ "This isn't about the product. It's about the woman who reaches for it every morning."
> ❌ "Shop now and experience the difference."

---

### ❌ DON'T — Voice

**DON'T use passive permission language.**
She doesn't need to be told to allow herself anything. She already has permission.

> ❌ "Allow yourself to feel beautiful."
> ❌ "Give yourself permission to indulge."
> ✅ "Feel beautiful. This is what that looks like."

---

**DON'T use exhausted empowerment language.**
"Boss babe," "slay," "you've got this," "empowering" — these words have been drained of meaning. They make the brand sound like every other feminine brand from 2019.

> ❌ "Slay in this boss babe collection."
> ✅ "For the woman who dresses like she's already decided."

---

**DON'T explain the emotion — show it.**
Telling the reader that something is "emotional" or "meaningful" without showing them how eliminates the emotion entirely.

> ❌ "This product creates an emotionally meaningful experience."
> ✅ "The kind of scent that makes you stand in the mirror a little longer."

---

**DON'T stack filler qualifiers.**
Very, really, quite, just, truly, actually — these words make confident copy sound hesitant. Remove all of them.

> ❌ "This is actually a really beautiful fragrance that you'll truly love."
> ✅ "This fragrance does exactly what it should. You'll know the moment you open it."

---

**DON'T use urgency or scarcity language.**
"Last chance," "don't miss out," "selling fast" — this is discount retailer language. It collapses the luxury positioning immediately.

> ❌ "Don't miss out! Last chance to shop the sale!"
> ✅ "Only a few remaining." or "Available for a limited time."

## SECTION 2 — VISUAL RULES

### ✅ DO — Visual

**DO use warm tones in every context.**
Every color choice — background, type, overlay, filter — leans warm. If it has a cool or grey undertone, it does not belong.

> ✅ Warm Ivory `#FAF6F0`, Soft Blush `#F2D4CC`, Espresso `#3B2314`
> ❌ Cool white, grey backgrounds, desaturated palettes, pure black

---

**DO let negative space work.**
Breathing room is not wasted space. It communicates luxury. Crowded compositions communicate budget.

> ✅ One strong image. One line of copy. Enough space to breathe.
> ❌ Four product images, three CTAs, two badges, and a banner — all competing.

---

**DO treat photography like emotional storytelling.**
Images should feel like a moment was captured — not staged. Warm light, intimate composition, sensory texture.

> ✅ Soft shadow across a product on cream linen. Warm rim light. Close enough to see texture.
> ❌ Flat lay on a white marble surface with harsh overhead light.

---

**DO use the full type system.**
Cormorant Garamond for display. Jost for body. Always. Both fonts must be present in any branded composition that includes type.

> ✅ Cormorant headline + Jost body + Jost label
> ❌ All sans-serif. All caps everywhere. Script fonts. System fonts.

---

**DO use Champagne Gold sparingly.**
One gold element per composition. It signals luxury because it is rare in the frame. Use it everywhere and it means nothing.

> ✅ Gold crown icon in the logo. Everything else in the palette.
> ❌ Gold buttons, gold borders, gold text, gold background section — all in one layout.

---

### ❌ DON'T — Visual

**DON'T use pure white or pure black.**
Pure white reads clinical. Pure black reads cold. Both disconnect from the brand's warmth.

> ❌ `#FFFFFF` background, `#000000` text
> ✅ `#FAF6F0` and `#3B2314` — always

---

**DON'T chase visual trends at the expense of identity.**
Trends are references, not directives. If a visual trend requires the brand to look like something other than MVQUEEN — it does not belong in the brand, regardless of what's performing on social.

> ❌ Adopting a cold dark-mode aesthetic because it's trending in luxury
> ✅ Interpreting a trend through MVQUEEN's warm, soft, cinematic lens

---

**DON'T overcrowd product imagery.**
More products in a single image does not mean more desire. It means less. Each product deserves its own moment.

> ❌ 8 products arranged in a collage-style flat lay
> ✅ One product, beautifully lit, with one supporting prop maximum

---

**DON'T use clip art, stock icon sets, or generic graphic elements.**
Decorative elements that could belong to any brand do not belong in MVQUEEN. Every graphic element is either part of the established visual system or it is removed.

> ❌ Generic star bursts, "NEW" stickers in Comic Sans energy, default Canva elements
> ✅ Clean typographic callouts, the crown icon, palette-consistent shape elements only

---

**DON'T put Miss.Princess palette colors in MVQUEEN primary contexts.**
Soft Lilac `#E8DCF0` and Petal Pink `#F7E0DA` belong to Miss.Princess. MVQUEEN uses Soft Blush and Dusty Rose. These are not the same.

## SECTION 3 — EXPERIENCE RULES

### ✅ DO — Experience

**DO treat every touchpoint as part of the product.**
The email subject line, the packaging tape, the order confirmation message, the social DM response — every interaction is MVQUEEN speaking. All of it is held to the same standard.

---

**DO make the unboxing feel intentional.**
Packaging is not functional protection — it is the first physical experience of the brand. Tissue paper, closing stickers, the weight of the box, the scent if applicable — all of it is considered.

> ✅ Warm Ivory mailer, Dusty Rose tissue, Espresso brand stamp, handwritten-style card insert
> ❌ Brown mailer, no tissue, printed packing slip only

---

**DO write customer communications in brand voice.**
Order confirmations, shipping notifications, return policies, customer service responses — all of it is written in MVQUEEN voice. Not corporate boilerplate.

> ✅ "Your order is on its way. We wrapped it with care."
> ❌ "Order #45621 has been processed and will ship within 3–5 business days."

---

**DO distinguish Miss.Princess clearly in every dual-brand context.**
When both brands appear in the same space, Miss.Princess is introduced as "our sister brand" and her visual identity is clearly differentiated — never blurred with MVQUEEN's.

---

### ❌ DON'T — Experience

**DON'T use shame, insecurity, or comparison as purchase triggers.**
MVQUEEN does not make women feel inadequate to sell them a solution. The brand elevates — it does not exploit.

> ❌ "Tired of dull skin? Finally fix it with..."
> ✅ "Skin that tells a story. This is the chapter where it glows."

---

**DON'T automate customer relationships without brand voice.**
No automated email, chatbot response, or AI-generated message goes live without a voice review. Automation at scale is not an excuse for cold, robotic communication.

---

**DON'T treat low-ticket items as less important.**
A $12 product receives the same copy quality, packaging consideration, and customer experience as a $95 product. Budget determines the materials — never the care.

---

**DON'T use the word "luxury" without earning it.**
The word luxury in MVQUEEN copy is only used when the sentence around it demonstrates what that luxury is. The word alone is not enough. Show it first.

> ❌ "Experience the luxury of MVQUEEN."
> ✅ "The weight of the bottle alone tells you something about what's inside."

## SECTION 4 — BRAND CONSISTENCY RULES

### The Five-Second Test
Any person who sees MVQUEEN content for the first time should be able to identify the brand within five seconds — not from the logo, but from the feeling. Warmth. Softness. Elegance. Confidence. If the content requires the logo to identify it as MVQUEEN, it is not yet brand-level content.

### The Mirror Test
Before any piece of content is published, ask: *Does this look or sound like any other brand?* If yes — it is not MVQUEEN content yet. Rewrite or redesign until it passes.

### The Brand Bible Test
If a content decision is in question, return to the Brand Bible. If it contradicts anything in the Brand Bible — it does not happen.

### The Forbidden Words Test
Run every piece of copy through `Forbidden_Words.md` before publishing. If a forbidden word is present — replace it. No exceptions.

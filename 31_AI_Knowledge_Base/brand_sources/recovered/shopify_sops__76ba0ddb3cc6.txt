# 📦 MVQUEEN — Shipping SOP

---

## Purpose

Define the standard operating procedure for all order fulfillment and shipping — ensuring every package that leaves MVQUEEN delivers a luxury experience from the moment it ships to the moment it arrives.

Shipping is not logistics. It is the last brand touchpoint before she holds the product.

---

## Shipping Standards

### Processing Time
| Order Type | Processing Time |
|-----------|----------------|
| Standard orders | 1-3 business days |
| Pre-orders | As stated on product page |
| High-volume periods (launches, holidays) | 3-5 business days — communicate proactively |

Processing time = time from order to handoff to carrier.
Never count weekends or holidays unless explicitly stated.

### Shipping Methods

| Method | Carrier | Estimated Transit | Cost |
|--------|---------|-----------------|------|
| Standard | USPS First Class / UPS Ground | 5-8 business days | Calculated at checkout |
| Expedited | USPS Priority / UPS 2-Day | 2-3 business days | Calculated at checkout |
| Express | UPS Overnight | 1 business day | Calculated at checkout |
| Free Shipping | USPS First Class | 5-8 business days | Orders over $[threshold] |

### International Shipping
- Available to: [list countries]
- Carrier: [carrier]
- Estimated transit: 10-21 business days
- Customs duties: Customer responsibility — stated clearly at checkout
- Tracking: Provided but may have gaps in international updates

---

## Fulfillment Process (Per Order)

```
□ 1. Order received — confirm payment cleared
□ 2. Pull inventory — verify item is in stock and undamaged
□ 3. Inspect product — quality check before packing
□ 4. Pack order per packaging standards (see below)
□ 5. Insert brand card and any promotional inserts
□ 6. Seal package securely
□ 7. Apply shipping label — verify address accuracy
□ 8. Scan/log order as fulfilled in Shopify
□ 9. Drop off at carrier or schedule pickup
□ 10. Confirm tracking number uploaded to Shopify order
□ 11. Shipping confirmation email auto-triggers — verify sent
```

---

## Packaging Standards

Every MVQUEEN package must reflect the brand:

| Element | Standard |
|---------|----------|
| Outer box/mailer | Branded, clean, premium feel |
| Inner wrap | Tissue paper — brand color |
| Brand card | Handwritten-style message or printed insert |
| Sticker/seal | Brand logo seal on tissue |
| Padding | Appropriate for product fragility |
| No excess | No cheap filler that undermines luxury feel |

**The unboxing test:** Would she want to photograph this? Would it feel special?
If no — repackage.

---

## Shipping Issue Protocols

### Lost Package (No tracking update 10+ days)
```
1. Verify shipping address was correct
2. Check carrier tracking for last known location
3. File carrier claim if eligible
4. Contact customer with empathy — offer replacement or refund
5. Do not make her wait more than 14 days for resolution
```

### Damaged Package
```
1. Request photo of damage from customer
2. Apologize immediately — no investigation theater
3. Send replacement within 2 business days
4. File carrier insurance claim if applicable
5. Log incident for packaging review
```

### Wrong Item Sent
```
1. Apologize immediately
2. Ship correct item with expedited shipping at no charge
3. Provide prepaid return label for wrong item
4. Do not require her to return item before sending correct one
```

---

## Shipping Notification Email Standard

Triggered automatically when tracking number is uploaded to Shopify.

**Tone:** Warm, anticipatory — never robotic.

Template: `17_Templates/T-02 Email Campaign Template`

Subject line example: *"She's on her way to you."*

# 🔄 MVQUEEN — Refund & Return SOP

---

## Purpose

Handle every return and refund with warmth, efficiency, and brand integrity — turning a potentially negative experience into proof that MVQUEEN stands behind what it sells.

The goal is never to minimize refunds by making them difficult.
The goal is to resolve every issue so well that she comes back anyway.

---

## Return Policy

| Item Type | Return Window | Condition Required |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Unopened products | 30 days from delivery | Original packaging, unused |
| Opened skincare/beauty | Not eligible | Hygiene reasons — stated at purchase |
| Clothing/fashion | 30 days | Unworn, tags attached |
| Accessories | 30 days | Unused, original packaging |
| Sale items | Final sale — no returns | Clearly stated at purchase |
| Damaged/defective | Any time | Photo required |

---

## Return Process

### Customer Initiates Return
```
□ Customer contacts via email or chat
□ Acknowledge within 4 hours (business hours)
□ Confirm eligibility against return policy
□ If eligible: provide return authorization and prepaid label
□ If not eligible: explain policy warmly, offer alternative resolution
```

### Return Received
```
□ Inspect returned item on arrival
□ Confirm condition matches what was stated
□ Process refund within 2 business days of receiving item
□ Send refund confirmation email
□ Update inventory in Shopify
□ Log return reason in database
```

### Refund Processing
| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---------------|----------------|
| Credit/debit card | 5-10 business days to appear |
| Shop Pay | 5-7 business days |
| PayPal | 3-5 business days |
| Store credit | Immediate |

---

## Refund Without Return (Exceptions)

Issue a refund or replacement WITHOUT requiring return when:
- Product arrived damaged (photo proof sufficient)
- Wrong item was sent
- Item lost in shipping and claim confirmed
- Order value under $30 (cost of return shipping exceeds value)
- Customer has strong purchase history (VIP — resolve immediately)

**Rule:** The cost of goodwill is almost always less than the cost of a lost customer.

---

## Refund Response Templates

**Eligible return:**
*"Of course — we want your experience with MVQUEEN to feel right. I'm sending your return label now. Once we receive the item, your refund will be processed within 2 business days. Thank you for giving us the chance to make this right. 🤍"*

**Ineligible return (opened beauty):**
*"We completely understand the frustration, and we're sorry the product didn't work as you hoped. Because of the nature of beauty products, we're unable to accept returns once opened — but we'd love to help you find the right product for you. Can you tell me a little more about what you were hoping for?"*

**Damaged product:**
*"We're so sorry this arrived damaged — that's not the experience we want for you at all. I'm sending a replacement out to you with priority shipping today. You don't need to return the damaged item."*

---

## Return Analytics

Track monthly:
- Total returns by product
- Return rate by category
- Top return reasons
- Return rate trend (should decrease as product quality improves)

High return rates on specific products trigger: product review, supplier audit, or listing accuracy check.

# 💬 MVQUEEN — Customer Support SOP

---

## Purpose

Deliver customer support so warm, efficient, and brand-aligned that every interaction — even a complaint — leaves the customer feeling better about MVQUEEN than before she reached out.

Customer support is not a cost center. It is a retention tool.

---

## Response Standards

| Channel | Response Target | Maximum |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| Email | Under 4 hours (business hours) | 24 hours |
| Instagram DM | Under 2 hours | 8 hours |
| TikTok comments | Under 4 hours | 12 hours |
| Website chat (if active) | Under 5 minutes | 30 minutes |

Business hours: [Define hours and timezone]

---

## The MVQUEEN Support Framework

Every customer interaction follows this arc:

```
ACKNOWLEDGE → UNDERSTAND → SOLVE → ELEVATE → CLOSE
```

**Acknowledge:** Validate her experience before solving anything.
Never open with a policy. Open with a human response.

**Understand:** Ask one clarifying question if needed.
Never make her explain herself multiple times.

**Solve:** Provide the clearest, most generous resolution available.
When in doubt — err toward the customer.

**Elevate:** Leave her feeling better than before she reached out.
A small unexpected gesture goes a long way.

**Close:** Warm, human, open door.
Never close a ticket in a way that feels like a door slamming.

---

## Issue Categories & Resolution Standards

| Issue | Resolution | Timeline |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Order status question | Provide tracking + ETA | Immediate |
| Late delivery | Acknowledge + provide carrier info + escalate if 10+ days | Same day |
| Damaged product | Replacement shipped, no return required | Same day |
| Wrong item | Correct item shipped expedited, prepaid return label sent | Same day |
| Lost package | Investigation + replacement or refund | Within 48 hours |
| Return request | Process per Refund SOP | Within 4 hours |
| Product question | Answer specifically + recommend if helpful | Within 4 hours |
| Complaint (general) | Listen, acknowledge, resolve generously | Within 2 hours |
| Negative review | Respond publicly, invite to DM, resolve privately | Within 1 hour |

---

## Response Templates

### Order Status
*"Your order is on its way! Here's your tracking number: [number]. Based on current carrier estimates, she should arrive by [date]. Let me know if you need anything else. 🤍"*

### Damaged Item
*"Oh no — I'm so sorry this happened. That's absolutely not the experience we want for you. I'm sending a replacement out to you today with priority shipping. You don't need to return the damaged item — just enjoy the new one when it arrives."*

### Late Delivery
*"We completely understand the frustration, and we're sorry for the wait. Your tracking shows [status]. Carrier delays are unfortunately outside our control, but we're watching it closely. If it doesn't update within [X] days, reach out and we'll make it right immediately."*

### Product Question
*"Great question! [Specific, helpful answer]. If you're also interested in [related product], it pairs beautifully with [product] for [specific reason]. Happy to help with anything else!"*

### Negative Public Comment
*"Thank you for sharing this — your experience matters to us. We'd love to make this right. Please DM us so we can take care of you personally."*

---

## Escalation Protocol

Escalate to founder immediately when:
- Customer threatens chargeback or legal action
- Order value is $150+ with unresolved issue
- Customer has 3+ orders and is experiencing a problem
- Issue involves product safety concern
- Situation is appearing publicly on social media

---

## Support Quality Standards

Monthly review:
- Average response time vs. target
- Resolution rate (resolved on first contact vs. multiple)
- Customer satisfaction (follow up with: "Did we resolve everything for you?")
- Return rate patterns indicating product issues
- Recurring questions indicating content/FAQ gap

# ⬆️ MVQUEEN — Upsell Systems

---

## Purpose

Increase average order value and customer lifetime value by introducing relevant, well-timed product recommendations that feel like personal suggestions — not sales tactics.

The MVQUEEN upsell philosophy:
**Recommend like a knowledgeable friend. Never push like a salesperson.**

---

## Upsell vs Cross-Sell

| Type | Definition | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Upsell | Upgrade to higher value version | Larger size, bundle, premium version |
| Cross-sell | Add complementary product | Moisturizer + SPF, dress + accessories |
| Post-purchase upsell | Offer after payment confirmed | One-click add-on at order confirmation |

---

## Upsell Placement Strategy

### Placement 1 — Product Page
**Type:** Cross-sell
**Format:** "Pairs beautifully with..." section
**Rules:**
- Maximum 3 product recommendations
- Must be genuinely complementary — not random
- Show with price
- Never interrupt the primary purchase flow

### Placement 2 — Cart Page
**Type:** Cross-sell + threshold upsell
**Format:**
- "You might also love..." — 1-2 complementary products
- Free shipping threshold bar: *"Add $X more for free shipping"*
**Rules:**
- One recommendation maximum in cart
- Threshold bar only if customer is within $20 of free shipping

### Placement 3 — Checkout Page
**Type:** Minimal — protect conversion
**Format:** None recommended — don't risk abandonment
**Rule:** Do not upsell on the checkout page. Focus only on completing the purchase.

### Placement 4 — Post-Purchase (Order Confirmation Page)
**Type:** One-click upsell
**Format:** *"Add [product] to your order before it ships — one tap, no re-entering payment."*
**Rules:**
- One product maximum
- Must be under $30 to reduce decision friction
- Complementary to what was just purchased
- Available for 15 minutes after purchase (before order is processed)

### Placement 5 — Post-Purchase Email (Day 14)
**Type:** Soft cross-sell
**Format:** *"You've had [product] for two weeks now — here's what she pairs perfectly with..."*
**Rules:**
- Never in the immediate post-purchase sequence (too soon)
- Must feel like a natural next step — not a sales email
- One product recommendation maximum

---

## Product Pairing Matrix

| Primary Product | Recommended Upsell | Recommended Cross-sell |
|----------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Skincare serum | Full-size version (if trial) | Moisturizer, SPF |
| Moisturizer | Bundle with cleanser | Serum, eye cream |
| Hair product | Full routine set | Styling product, hair accessories |
| Dress | Size up bundle | Accessories, shoes |
| Accessories | Set / bundle | Complementary accessory |
| Fragrance | Larger size | Body lotion in same scent |

*(Expand with real product pairings as catalog grows)*

---

## Bundle Architecture

Bundles increase AOV while giving the customer perceived value.

**Bundle types:**
| Bundle Type | Discount | Notes |
|------------|---------|-------|
| Starter Ritual | 10% off | 2-3 entry products |
| Full Ritual | 15% off | Complete product routine |
| Gift Set | 10% off + gift packaging | Seasonal, giftable |
| Build Your Own | 10% off when buying 3+ | Flexible, high conversion |

**Bundle pricing rule:**
Never discount more than 20% — protects margin and luxury positioning.

---

## Upsell Copy Standards

Upsell language must feel like a recommendation — not a push.

**✅ Use:**
- *"Pairs beautifully with..."*
- *"Complete the ritual with..."*
- *"Women who love [product] also reach for..."*
- *"Her natural companion..."*

**❌ Never use:**
- *"You might also want to buy..."*
- *"Don't forget to add..."*
- *"Frequently bought together"* (Amazon language)
- *"Limited time — add now!"*

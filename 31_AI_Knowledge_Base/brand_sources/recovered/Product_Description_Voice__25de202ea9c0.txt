# 👑 MVQUEEN PRODUCT DESCRIPTION VOICE
*The complete system for writing product descriptions that feel like MVQUEEN*

---

## PURPOSE

MVQUEEN product descriptions lead with feeling. They connect the product to her identity. By the time she's finished reading, she doesn't just know what the product is — she knows how it will make her feel. And that is why she buys.

---

## THE 5-PART FORMULA

PART 1: THE EMOTIONAL HOOK — One sentence. One feeling. Her desire or identity, not the product.
PART 2: THE SENSORY STORY — 2–4 sentences. How it looks, feels, smells, moves. She imagines herself with it.
PART 3: THE FUNCTIONAL TRUTH — 2–3 bullets. Real ingredients, materials, sizes, colors. Precise, never clinical.
PART 4: THE IDENTITY STAMP — One sentence. She sees herself in it.
PART 5: THE INVITATION — One line. Soft CTA energy — an invitation, not a command.

## TEMPLATES BY CATEGORY

FRAGRANCE:
Hook: [Emotional desire / identity statement about scent and presence]
Sensory: [Notes described in feelings and atmospheres, not just ingredients]
Functional: Top/Heart/Base notes · Size · Concentration · Longevity
Identity: [Who this fragrance is for — lifestyle, not demographics]
Invitation: [One soft closing line]

EXAMPLE: Some scents announce you. This one becomes you. / A fragrance for the woman who enters a room quietly and leaves an impression that stays. Opens with fresh bergamot and soft peony. Heart: rose and jasmine. Base: warm amber, sandalwood, vanilla — intimate, lingering, undeniably hers. / Top: Bergamot, Peony · Heart: Rose, Jasmine · Base: Amber, Sandalwood, Vanilla · 50ml/100ml EDP · 6–8hrs / For the woman who wants to be remembered — without saying a word. / Wear it like you already know.

SKINCARE:
Hook: [How her skin feels vs. how she wants it to feel]
Sensory: [Texture, application, visible results, ritual moment]
Functional: Key ingredients · Skin type · AM/PM · How to use · Size
Identity: [Self-care lifestyle lens]
Invitation: [Soft closing line]

EXAMPLE: She started using this serum and her skin started to glow. Then she started to glow. / A weightless, water-silk formula that absorbs in seconds and works while she sleeps. Morning skin: rested, luminous, plumped from the inside. / Hyaluronic Acid, Vitamin C, Niacinamide · All skin types · AM & PM · 3–4 drops on clean skin · 30ml / For the woman who treats her skincare like a ritual — because it is one. / Your glow was always in there. This just helped it surface.

---

FASHION:
Hook: [The feeling of wearing this — not the look]
Sensory: [How fabric moves. How it fits. What occasion it lives in through lifestyle language]
Functional: Fabric · Fit · Colors (MVQUEEN color language) · Sizes XS–3X · Care
Identity: [How she moves in the world wearing this]
Invitation: [Soft closing line]

EXAMPLE: The dress that makes her feel like she dressed for herself — and ended up perfect for every room she walked into. / Fluid satin midi, wrap silhouette. Moves when she moves. In champagne she looks like a Sunday morning. In queen noir, she looks like a decision. / 95% polyester satin, 5% elastane · Wrap midi, adjustable tie waist · Champagne, Blush, Queen Noir · XS–3X · Hand wash cold / For the woman who is always dressed for something — even when nothing's planned. / Wear it to dinner. Wear it to wherever she's going next.

BEAUTY / MAKEUP:
Hook: [What this product gives her — confidence, presence, a feeling]
Sensory: [Texture, finish, application, performance through the day]
Functional: Finish · Formula · Shade range · Size · Longevity
Identity: [Her relationship with this product through identity, not habit]
Invitation: [Soft closing line]

EXAMPLE: Her lipstick has always said things her voice didn't have to. / Velvet finish. Feels like nothing on her lips — until she looks in the mirror. Full pigment, one swipe. No feathering. Stays through coffee, conversation, day, night. / Velvet matte · Long-wear, non-drying, cruelty-free · Blush Nude, Rose Queen, Berry Reign, Deep Plum · 3.5g · 8+ hours / For the woman who doesn't reapply — because she didn't need to. / Pick one. Or don't choose at all.

JEWELRY:
Hook: [The emotional reason she buys jewelry — for herself, not anyone else]
Sensory: [How it looks on. The weight. The catch of light. Layering potential]
Functional: Material · Plating (18K/14K) · Dimensions · What's included · Care
Identity: [Her energy and intention]
Invitation: [Soft closing line]

EXAMPLE: She used to wait for someone to give her gold. Then she bought it for herself. / Three delicate chains — different lengths, same luminous gold. Lightweight enough to forget. Layered, they catch every light in the room. Worn alone, understated and deliberate. / Brass with 18K gold plating · 16", 18", 20" · 3-piece set · Nickel-free, tarnish-resistant · Store in pouch, avoid water / For the woman who is her own most thoughtful gift-giver. / Add all three. Because she deserves the full set.

## LANGUAGE RULES

WORDS THAT ELEVATE:
Texture/Feel: silky · velvet · weightless · fluid · soft · luminous · warm · delicate · rich · smooth
Presence/Effect: lingers · settles · catches light · moves · transforms · unfolds · deepens · reveals
Confidence/Identity: deliberate · intentional · effortless · signature · hers · worthy · elevated · feminine

NEVER USE: "Affordable" · "Amazing" · "Incredible" · "Stunning" · "High quality" · "Perfect for any occasion" · "You'll love it!" · "Buy now" · "Cheap" · "Budget"

---

## LENGTH GUIDELINES

Short (collection thumbnail): 1–2 sentences, hook only
Standard (product page): 150–250 words
Extended (hero/launch): 250–400 words
Email feature: 50–100 words
Social caption: 2–5 sentences + CTA

---

## THE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION TEST

1. Does it open with feeling, not feature?
2. Does she imagine herself with it by sentence 3?
3. Does it include real product information?
4. Does it connect to her identity — not just her wardrobe or shelf?
5. Does it sound like MVQUEEN, not a generic e-commerce template?

If any answer is NO — rewrite before publishing.

---

*Product descriptions are the brand's most intimate conversation with her. She is alone, reading, deciding. Make it feel like the brand already knows who she is.*

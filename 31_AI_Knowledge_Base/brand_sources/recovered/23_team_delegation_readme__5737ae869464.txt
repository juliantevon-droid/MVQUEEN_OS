# 👥 MVQUEEN — Team & Delegation System

---

## Purpose

The Team & Delegation System governs how MVQUEEN builds, manages, and scales its human infrastructure. It defines who does what, how work is delegated, and how the brand maintains quality and consistency as the team grows.

A brand is only as strong as the people executing it.
Every person who touches MVQUEEN — whether full-time, part-time, freelance, or AI-assisted — must understand the doctrine before they touch the work.

---

## Team Philosophy

MVQUEEN does not hire bodies — it builds collaborators.

Every team member, contractor, and partner is:
- Briefed on brand doctrine before starting
- Given clear ownership of their role
- Measured by output quality — not just hours
- Trusted to execute within defined standards
- Supported with systems — not micromanaged

The goal is a lean, high-performing team
where every role is essential and every person is empowered.

## Role Definitions

### Virtual Assistant (First Hire Priority)
**Owns:** Admin, order management, customer service, basic content scheduling
**Does not own:** Brand decisions, creative direction, strategy
**Hours:** 10-20hrs/week to start
**Tools needed:** Shopify access, email platform, social scheduling tool
**Onboarding requirement:** Read brand manifesto, tone guide, customer service SOP

### Content Creator
**Owns:** Social content creation, caption writing, visual production
**Does not own:** Brand strategy, product decisions, campaign direction
**Deliverables:** 5-7 posts/week across platforms
**Tools needed:** Brand asset library, content calendar, tone guide
**Onboarding requirement:** Full tone guide + visual identity review

### Customer Service Rep
**Owns:** All customer inquiries, order issues, returns, reviews
**Does not own:** Refund policy exceptions, pricing decisions
**Response standard:** Under 4 hours during business hours
**Tools needed:** Shopify, email, brand voice script library
**Onboarding requirement:** Customer service SOP + brand voice training

### Marketing Manager
**Owns:** Campaign execution, ad management, email marketing, influencer coordination
**Does not own:** Brand positioning, product decisions, budget allocation
**Deliverables:** Monthly campaign calendar, weekly performance report
**Tools needed:** Meta Ads, email platform, analytics dashboard
**Onboarding requirement:** Full brand strategy review + marketing system docs

## Delegation Framework

### What to Delegate First
Prioritize delegating tasks that are:
- Repetitive and process-driven
- Time-consuming but not strategy-dependent
- Clearly documentable with an SOP
- Not requiring brand judgment calls

**Delegate first:**
- Order fulfillment coordination
- Customer service responses (with scripts)
- Content scheduling (not creation)
- Data entry and reporting
- Supplier follow-ups
- Social media engagement (comments/DMs)

**Keep until you can hire right:**
- Brand creative direction
- Product selection and sourcing
- Campaign strategy
- Partnership decisions
- Financial oversight

## Onboarding System

Every new team member completes this before starting:

| Step | Document | Time |
|------|---------|------|
| 1 | Read brand manifesto | 15 min |
| 2 | Read tone & voice guide | 20 min |
| 3 | Review brand identity system | 20 min |
| 4 | Review their role SOP | 30 min |
| 5 | Shadow founder for one work session | 1-2 hrs |
| 6 | Complete first task with feedback | Varies |
| 7 | Sign confidentiality agreement | 10 min |

No one starts executing without completing onboarding.

## Contractor vs Employee Decision

| Situation | Recommendation |
|-----------|---------------|
| Under 20hrs/week needed | Contractor / Freelancer |
| Ongoing, specialized role | Contractor long-term |
| Core business function, 30+hrs | Consider employee |
| Sensitive data access needed | Employee preferred |
| Creative work, project-based | Freelancer |

**Rule:** All contractors sign an NDA and independent contractor agreement before accessing brand systems.

---

## Communication Standards

| Channel | Purpose | Response Time |
|---------|---------|--------------|
| Email | Formal communication, briefs, agreements | 24 hours |
| WhatsApp / Telegram | Quick updates, urgent items | 4 hours |
| Shared docs | Ongoing project collaboration | Async |
| Weekly check-in | Progress review and alignment | Scheduled |

**Rule:** All brand feedback is documented in writing — not just verbally communicated.

---

## Brand Protection with Team

Every collaborator must:
- Sign NDA covering brand strategy, product sourcing, and customer data
- Use only approved brand assets from the asset library
- Never post about MVQUEEN without approval (unless it's their job)
- Never share pricing, supplier, or margin information
- Return or delete all brand materials upon contract end

---

## CTA PRINCIPLES

1. Invitation over command — CTAs invite, never bark orders
2. Identity-forward language — connects to who she is or who she's becoming
3. Specificity over generic — tells her exactly what world she's stepping into
4. Softness and confidence in balance — holds the door open, never pushes

## CTA TONE REFERENCE

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero banner | Confident, aspirational | "Enter the World of MVQUEEN" |
| Product page | Warm, direct | "Make It Mine" |
| Email signup | Inviting, exclusive | "Join the Reign" |
| Social caption | Conversational, feminine | "Link in bio, queen." |
| Abandoned cart | Warm, no pressure | "Your Bag Is Still Waiting" |
| Sale/promo | Elevated, never desperate | "Yours — While She Lasts" |
| VIP/loyalty | Exclusive, celebratory | "You've Earned This" |
| Re-engagement | Warm, personal | "We Saved Something for You" |

---

## WORDS NEVER USED IN CTAs
❌ "Click here" · ❌ "Buy now" (use "Shop Now" minimum) · ❌ "Hurry!" / "Act fast!" · ❌ "Cheap" / "Affordable" / "Bargain" · ❌ "You won't regret it" · ❌ "What are you waiting for?"

---

*Every CTA is a moment of brand connection. It should feel like an invitation from someone who already knows she belongs here. Because she does.*

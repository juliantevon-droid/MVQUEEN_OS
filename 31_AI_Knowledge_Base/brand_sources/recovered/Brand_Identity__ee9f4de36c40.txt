## Color Usage Rules

### Backgrounds
- **Digital primary:** Ivory Cream (`#F8F4EE`) or Warm White (`#FDFAF5`)
- **Never:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) — reads cold and clinical
- **Never:** Pure black — contradicts brand warmth entirely
- **Dark option (for contrast):** Warm Dark (`#2C2420`) used sparingly

### Accent Color Hierarchy
1. Champagne — most used accent (borders, dividers, warm highlights)
2. Blush Rose — feminine accent (CTAs, highlights, feminine content)
3. Muted Gold — luxury accent (use sparingly — only when it earns its place)
4. Sage — wellness/nature content only
5. Mauve — fashion/editorial only

### Color Proportion Rule
```
70% — Primary palette (Ivory, Warm White, Champagne)
20% — Secondary accent (Blush, Sage, Mauve, Taupe)
10% — Gold (luxury signal — never overuse)
```

---

## Platform-Specific Color Application

### Instagram Feed
- Background tone: Ivory Cream dominant
- Accent: Blush Rose and Champagne for warmth
- Text overlays: Warm Dark on light backgrounds, Off-White on dark
- Consistency: All posts should feel cohesive in a grid view

### Email
- Header background: Ivory Cream
- Body background: Warm White
- Accent dividers: Champagne
- CTA buttons: Blush Rose or Warm Dark
- Footer: Deep Cream

### Website
- Page background: Warm White
- Section backgrounds: Alternate Ivory Cream and Warm White
- CTA buttons: Blush Rose (primary), Warm Dark (secondary)
- Borders and dividers: Champagne
- Sale/alert badges: Dusty Mauve (never red — too aggressive)

### Packaging
- Primary box: Ivory Cream (matte)
- Logo: Muted Gold foil or Warm Dark deboss
- Tissue: Blush Rose or Ivory
- Seal sticker: Muted Gold on Ivory

### Pinterest
- Pin backgrounds: Ivory, Warm White, Champagne dominant
- Text overlays: Warm Dark — always legible
- Accent: Blush for pinnable appeal

## Color Accessibility Standards

Text on background must meet WCAG AA contrast requirements:

| Text Color | Background | Contrast Ratio | Pass/Fail |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|----------|
| `#2C2420` on `#F8F4EE` | Warm Dark on Ivory | 12.8:1 | ✅ AAA |
| `#2C2420` on `#FDFAF5` | Warm Dark on Warm White | 13.5:1 | ✅ AAA |
| `#F8F4EE` on `#2C2420` | Off-White on Warm Dark | 12.8:1 | ✅ AAA |
| `#C9A96E` on `#F8F4EE` | Muted Gold on Ivory | 3.1:1 | ⚠️ Use large text only |

**Rule:** Never use Muted Gold as small body text — contrast is insufficient.

---

## Seasonal Color Shifts

The core palette stays consistent year-round. Seasonal content uses accent shifts:

| Season | Accent Shift |
|--------|------------|
| Spring | Introduce Pale Lavender and fresh Sage |
| Summer | Warmer Champagne, lighter Blush |
| Autumn | Deeper Warm Taupe, richer Champagne |
| Winter | Muted Gold amplified, Deep Cream backgrounds |

Core palette never changes — only accent emphasis shifts.

# ✍️ MVQUEEN — Typography System

---

## Purpose

The definitive typography system for MVQUEEN — governing font selection, hierarchy, sizing, spacing, and usage across all digital and print applications.

Typography is voice made visible. Every font choice communicates something about the brand before a word is read.

---

## Typography Philosophy

MVQUEEN typography must feel:
- **Feminine** without being delicate or unreadable
- **Luxurious** without being inaccessible or cold
- **Modern** without being trend-dependent
- **Warm** — never sterile or clinical

The typography system creates a reading experience that feels like inhabiting the brand — unhurried, considered, and beautiful.

---

## Font System

### Display / Headline Font
**Role:** Headlines, campaign statements, hero text, brand declarations
**Character:** Elegant, feminine, distinctive — creates the brand's visual voice
**Type style:** Serif with warmth — classic proportions, modern refinement

**Recommended options:**
| Font | Style | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Cormorant Garamond | Serif | Free via Google Fonts — elegant, feminine |
| Playfair Display | Serif | Free via Google Fonts — high contrast, luxurious |
| Libre Baskerville | Serif | Free — refined, readable at all sizes |

**Usage:** Headlines H1, H2 on hero sections, campaign statements, packaging brand name

---

### Body / Supporting Font
**Role:** Body copy, product descriptions, captions, navigation, UI text
**Character:** Clean, warm, highly readable — supports the headline without competing
**Type style:** Sans-serif with humanist qualities — never cold or geometric

**Recommended options:**
| Font | Style | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Jost | Sans-serif | Free — clean, warm, excellent readability |
| DM Sans | Sans-serif | Free — humanist, modern, warm |
| Nunito | Sans-serif | Free — rounded, approachable, readable |

**Usage:** All body copy, navigation, buttons, captions, email body text

---

### Accent / Script Font (Use Sparingly)
**Role:** Special moments only — taglines, packaging inserts, brand card messages
**Character:** Handwritten quality — personal and warm
**Type style:** Script or handwritten

**Recommended options:**
| Font | Style | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Playfair Display Italic | Italic serif | Elegant substitute for script |
| Dancing Script | Script | Free — readable handwritten feel |

**Usage:** Brand card messages, packaging accents, special campaign moments ONLY
**Rule:** Maximum one instance per design. Never in body copy. Never for navigation.

---

## Type Hierarchy

### Digital (Website & Email)

| Level | Font | Size | Weight | Color | Use |
|-------|------|------|--------|-------|-----|
| H1 | Display | 36-48px | Regular | Warm Dark | Page headlines, hero text |
| H2 | Display | 28-36px | Regular | Warm Dark | Section headlines |
| H3 | Display or Body | 22-28px | Regular/Medium | Warm Dark | Subsection headers |
| H4 | Body | 18-20px | Medium | Warm Dark | Card titles, product names |
| Body Large | Body | 16-18px | Regular | Warm Dark | Lead paragraphs |
| Body | Body | 14-16px | Regular | Warm Dark | Standard body copy |
| Caption | Body | 12-13px | Regular | Warm Grey | Image captions, metadata |
| Button | Body | 14-15px | Medium | Variable | CTA buttons |
| Navigation | Body | 14-15px | Regular | Warm Dark | Site navigation |

### Mobile Adjustments (Reduce all sizes by ~2px)

| Level | Mobile Size |
|-------|------------|
| H1 | 28-36px |
| H2 | 22-28px |
| Body | 14-16px |

---

## Spacing Standards

**Line height:**
- Headlines: 1.2-1.3 (tighter for elegance)
- Body text: 1.6-1.8 (looser for readability)
- Captions: 1.4

**Letter spacing:**
- Headlines: -0.02em to 0 (slightly tight for luxury feel)
- Body: 0 to 0.01em (normal)
- All caps / labels: 0.08-0.12em (always letter-space all caps)
- Captions: 0.02-0.04em

**Paragraph spacing:**
- Between paragraphs: 1.5 × line height
- After headers: 0.5 × line height

---

## Typography Rules

**Rule 1 — Never more than two fonts in any single design.**
Display font + Body font. The script accent font is a rare third when absolutely needed.

**Rule 2 — Never use all-caps for body copy.**
All-caps is acceptable for short labels, navigation items, and button text. Never for full sentences or paragraphs.

**Rule 3 — Minimum body text size is 14px on digital.**
Below 14px is inaccessible and signals poor design quality.

**Rule 4 — Generous line height in body copy.**
Tight line height in body copy makes reading feel effortful. 1.6-1.8 is the minimum for comfort.

**Rule 5 — Headlines are never bold in MVQUEEN.**
Regular weight serif headlines signal refinement. Bold headlines signal loudness — which is off-brand.

**Rule 6 — Left-align body text.**
Center alignment is acceptable for very short decorative text. Never center-align paragraphs — it reduces readability and feels amateur.

---

## Email Typography Specifics

Email clients have limited font support. Use web-safe fallbacks:

| MVQUEEN Font | Email Fallback |
|-------------|--------------|
| Cormorant Garamond | Georgia, serif |
| Playfair Display | Georgia, serif |
| Jost | Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif |
| DM Sans | Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif |

**Email font stack example:**
`font-family: 'Jost', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;`

# 📏 MVQUEEN — Brand Rules

---

## Purpose

The non-negotiable standards governing how the MVQUEEN brand is expressed — across visuals, language, partnerships, and operations. These rules protect the brand's integrity as it scales.

This is not a suggestion list. These are rules.
Deviation requires explicit founder approval.

---

## Visual Brand Rules

### Logo
```
Rule 1: The MVQUEEN logo is never stretched, distorted, or recolored outside approved palette.
Rule 2: Minimum clear space around logo = height of the "M" on all sides.
Rule 3: Logo on light backgrounds: Warm Dark (#2C2420) or Muted Gold (#C9A96E).
Rule 4: Logo on dark backgrounds: Off-White (#F8F4EE) or Muted Gold (#C9A96E).
Rule 5: Never place logo on busy photography without sufficient contrast.
Rule 6: Never use drop shadows, outlines, or effects on the logo.
```

### Color
```
Rule 7: All brand colors come from the MVQUEEN Color System — no improvised colors.
Rule 8: Pure white (#FFFFFF) and pure black (#000000) are not MVQUEEN colors.
Rule 9: Muted Gold is used sparingly — it signals luxury only when not overused.
Rule 10: No neon, bright, or high-saturation colors anywhere in the brand ecosystem.
```

### Photography
```
Rule 11: All photography must align with MVQUEEN Visual Identity Philosophy.
Rule 12: Harsh flash lighting, stark shadows, and cold color grading are prohibited.
Rule 13: All photography is post-processed with warm color grading — never cool/blue tones.
Rule 14: Stock photography is never used for hero/primary content — only for filler if needed.
Rule 15: AI-generated images must pass brand visual review before publication.
```

### Typography
```
Rule 16: Only fonts from the MVQUEEN Typography System are used in brand materials.
Rule 17: Maximum two fonts per design (display + body). Script accent is a rare third.
Rule 18: Headlines are never bold. Regular weight serif only.
Rule 19: Body text minimum 14px on all digital applications.
Rule 20: All-caps only for short labels — never for sentences or paragraphs.
```

---

## Language Brand Rules

### Voice
```
Rule 21: Every piece of content passes the Tone Guide before publication.
Rule 22: No words from the Forbidden Words list appear in any customer-facing content.
Rule 23: AI-generated content is reviewed by a human before publishing — no exceptions.
Rule 24: Content never implies a woman needs to change to deserve MVQUEEN.
Rule 25: Marketing never uses comparison, insecurity, or fear as conversion tactics.
```

### Formatting
```
Rule 26: Multiple exclamation marks (!! or !!!) never appear in brand copy.
Rule 27: ALL CAPS words in body copy are prohibited.
Rule 28: Aggressive urgency language ("Buy NOW", "Don't miss out!", "Last chance!!") is prohibited.
Rule 29: Generic CTAs ("Click here", "Buy now") are replaced with CTA Library options.
Rule 30: Emojis are used selectively — maximum 1-2 per post, never in formal content.
```

---

## Partnership Brand Rules

```
Rule 31: No partnership proceeds without a review against brand alignment criteria.
Rule 32: All influencer content includes legal FTC disclosure (#ad or #sponsored).
Rule 33: No partner uses MVQUEEN assets without a signed agreement.
Rule 34: MVQUEEN never endorses a competitor's product — explicitly or implicitly.
Rule 35: Partnership content that contradicts MVQUEEN values is grounds for immediate contract termination.
```

---

## Pricing Brand Rules

```
Rule 36: MVQUEEN never runs flash sales or countdown-timer discount promotions.
Rule 37: Discounts over 30% are prohibited — they undermine luxury positioning.
Rule 38: "Sale" language is replaced with: "At a gentler price" or "Selected at [price]."
Rule 39: Free shipping threshold is maintained and communicated — not hidden.
Rule 40: Price increases are introduced gradually with quality signaling — never abrupt.
```

---

## Digital Brand Rules

```
Rule 41: The MVQUEEN website is mobile-optimized and tested on mobile before every launch.
Rule 42: Page load time target is under 3 seconds on mobile — this is a brand standard.
Rule 43: All product images have alt text before products go live.
Rule 44: All customer data is handled per privacy policy — no exceptions.
Rule 45: Broken links are fixed within 48 hours of identification.
```

---

## AI Brand Rules

```
Rule 46: No AI output is published without human brand voice review.
Rule 47: All AI prompts used for customer-facing content are loaded with brand voice context.
Rule 48: AI systems operate within brand doctrine — they do not override it.
Rule 49: AI-generated images are reviewed against visual identity standards before use.
Rule 50: The AI Prompt Library is updated when brand voice evolves.
```

---

## The Override Clause

If any rule creates a conflict with a specific business situation — bring it to the founder.

Rules exist to protect the brand. The founder exists to make judgment calls when rules create unintended friction.

No team member, collaborator, or AI system has authority to override these rules independently.

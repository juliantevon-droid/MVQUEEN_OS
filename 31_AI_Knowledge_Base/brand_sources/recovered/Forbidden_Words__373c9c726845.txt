# 🚫 MVQUEEN — Forbidden Words, Phrases & Patterns

---

## Why This File Exists

Language is the first thing a customer experiences before she ever holds a product.
The wrong word doesn't just sound off — it breaks the world MVQUEEN is building.
It makes the brand feel like every other brand. And MVQUEEN is not every other brand.

This file is law.
It applies to every writer, every AI agent, every caption, every email, every product description, every campaign, every SMS, every customer support message — without exception.

If a word is on this list, find the alternative. They are provided below.

---

## SECTION 1 — Hard Forbidden: Never Use

These words and phrases have been permanently removed from the MVQUEEN vocabulary. They are either emotionally hollow, culturally overused, brand-inconsistent, or they pull the language into a register that cheapens the world we're building.

### Hustle & Grind Culture Language
| Forbidden | Why | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Boss babe | Hollow, overused, reductive | Ambitious woman / She moves with intention |
| Girl boss | Expired, patronizing | Driven / Self-directed / Quietly powerful |
| Hustle | Contradicts softness philosophy | Intentional work / Building with purpose |
| Grind | Harsh, masculine-coded effort | Commitment / Consistent elevation |
| Slay | Overused to meaninglessness | Radiate / Command / Own the moment |
| Killing it | Violent undertone, exhausted phrase | Thriving / Moving with grace |
| Werk | Trend-dependent, dates immediately | — (don't replace, remove) |
| Girlboss energy | Double violation | — (remove entirely) |

| Deal | Strips the luxury positioning entirely | Investment / Value |

## SECTION 2 — Overuse Restrictions

These words are not forbidden — they are *rationed*. Each one has earned its place in the MVQUEEN vocabulary. But because they appear so frequently in the Brand Bible and other OS files, they must be used with intention, not as defaults.

| Word / Phrase | The Restriction |
|---|---|
| **Emotional / Emotionally** | Maximum 2 uses per 300 words. When you feel yourself reaching for it, ask: *what specific emotion?* Name it instead. |
| **Feminine / Femininity** | Beautiful word — but it can carry every sentence if you let it. Let visuals and context do the work sometimes. |
| **Elevated / Elevation** | Reserve for moments where the *uplift* is the actual point. Not as a filler adjective. |
| **Intentional / Intentionally** | Powerful when earned. Meaningless when applied to everything. |
| **Immersive** | Use to describe *experiences* — not products, not people, not basic interactions. |
| **Softness / Soft** | This is core brand DNA — but three "soft" in one paragraph flattens it. Vary with: gentle, warm, delicate, unhurried, quiet. |
| **Radiant / Luminous** | Cosmetics favorites. Earn them. Don't assign them to everything that glows. |
| **Confidence** | The brand's core promise. Use it where it lands hardest, not everywhere. |
| **Atmosphere** | Excellent brand word. Use it for spaces, moods, energy — not as a synonym for "vibe." |
| **Curated** | Acceptable once per piece of copy. After that, it sounds like you're afraid to commit to a specific choice. |
| **Authentic / Authenticity** | Rarely needed — if the copy is actually authentic, the word is unnecessary. |

## SECTION 3 — Patterns to Avoid

Beyond individual words, these sentence and structural patterns weaken MVQUEEN copy:

**1. The Definition Opening**
> *"Luxury is more than just a price tag..."*
Do not open copy by defining what something is. Show it instead.

**2. The List as Thought**
> *"This product is: beautiful, feminine, elevated, intentional."*
Bullet lists of adjectives are not copy. Write a sentence.

**3. The Obvious Relatability Play**
> *"We know life gets busy..."*
MVQUEEN does not bond with customers over stress and exhaustion. We offer escape from it.

**4. Passive Permission Language**
> *"Allow yourself to feel beautiful."*
MVQUEEN women do not need permission. Write: *Feel beautiful. You already are.*

**5. The Double Emotional Qualifier**
> *"Emotionally immersive and deeply emotional..."*
If both words mean the same thing, one of them is filler. Cut it.

**6. Over-Punctuated Pause Lines**
> *"Softness. Elegance. Confidence. That's MVQUEEN."*
Used sparingly, this structure is powerful. Used in every piece of copy, it becomes a tic. Earn the pause.

**7. Vague Transformation Language**
> *"Transform your routine."*
Into what? From what? Be specific. Transformation is only believable when it's concrete.

## SECTION 4 — Miss.Princess Exception Rules

Miss.Princess operates in a different register — younger, more playful, more trend-aware. Some forbidden MVQUEEN words are permitted in Miss.Princess copy with limits:

| Word | MVQUEEN | Miss.Princess |
|---|---|---|
| Slay | Never | Sparingly — max 1x per piece |
| Main character | Never | Allowed in caption hooks |
| Era | Never | Allowed once per campaign |
| Cute | Rarely (too casual) | Freely — it's core to the brand |
| Girly | Never | Allowed with intention |
| Trendy | Never | Allowed when referencing specific trends |

Miss.Princess is not exempt from the hard-forbidden list. No cheap urgency. No corporate speak. No fake luxury language.

## Quick Reference Card — 10 Words to Always Replace

When you catch yourself writing these, stop and rewrite:

1. **Empowering** → Show what confidence feels like
2. **Affordable** → Thoughtfully priced / Attainable
3. **Buy now** → Make it yours / Shop the collection
4. **Boss babe** → Remove entirely
5. **Emotional** (as reflex) → Name the specific emotion
6. **Self-love** → Self-connection / Intentional care
7. **Luxurious** (as crutch) → Describe what makes it feel that way
8. **Curated** (more than once) → Be specific about what and why
9. **Level up** → Elevate / Step into more
10. **You deserve it** → This was always meant for you

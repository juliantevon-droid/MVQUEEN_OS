# 🔥 MVQUEEN — Viral Hooks

---

## Purpose

A library of proven viral hook formulas, real examples, and testing frameworks for creating content that spreads — while staying unmistakably MVQUEEN.

Viral content at MVQUEEN is never accidental.
It is engineered from emotional truth, not shock value.

---

## What Makes Content Go Viral

Content spreads when it creates one of these reactions:
1. **Recognition** — *"That's exactly me."*
2. **Revelation** — *"I never thought of it that way."*
3. **Resonance** — *"She said what I couldn't say."*
4. **Utility** — *"I need to save this."*
5. **Beauty** — *"I need to share this."*

Every MVQUEEN viral hook triggers at least one of these.

---

## Viral Hook Formulas by Platform

### TikTok / Reels Hooks (First 2 seconds)

**Formula V1 — The Painful Truth**
*"Nobody tells you that [uncomfortable truth about beauty/self-care/femininity]"*

Examples:
- *"Nobody tells you that most skincare routines are designed to make you dependent, not healed."*
- *"Nobody tells you that 'treat yourself' culture was designed to make you feel guilty until you spend."*
- *"Nobody tells you that your morning routine is supposed to restore you, not exhaust you."*

---

**Formula V2 — The Hot Take**
*"[Controversial but true opinion about beauty/luxury/femininity]"*

Examples:
- *"Expensive skincare isn't the problem. Not knowing what you actually need is."*
- *"The 'soft life' trend missed the point entirely."*
- *"Most luxury brands don't actually like their customers."*

---

**Formula V3 — The Specific Permission**
*"If you [very specific scenario she recognizes] — this is for you."*

Examples:
- *"If you've been buying skincare for a year and still don't love your skin — this is for you."*
- *"If your morning routine feels like a chore instead of a ritual — this is for you."*
- *"If you've been waiting for a brand that treats you like you have taste — this is for you."*

---

**Formula V4 — The Story Open**
*"I used to [relatable struggle] until [turning point]."*

Examples:
- *"I used to spend $400 on skincare and still feel nothing until I realized what I was actually missing."*
- *"I used to think luxury wasn't for women like me until I understood what luxury actually means."*

---

**Formula V5 — The Comparison Flip**
*"[Expected outcome] vs [unexpected better outcome]"*

Examples:
- *"Expensive routine vs intentional routine — here's what actually changes your skin."*
- *"Beauty as performance vs beauty as restoration — the difference is everything."*

---

## Viral Content Formats

### Format 1 — The Revelation List
*"X things I wish someone told me about [topic]"*
- 3-7 items
- Each one specific and actionable
- Last item is most powerful — save the best for the end

### Format 2 — The Before/After (Not Physical)
Show the emotional or lifestyle transformation — not physical appearance.
*"My beauty routine before MVQUEEN / after MVQUEEN"*
Focus: How she feels, how mornings feel, her relationship with beauty

### Format 3 — The POV
*"POV: You finally found a brand that was built for you"*
Immersive, second-person, atmospheric

### Format 4 — The Aesthetic Day-in-the-Life
No voiceover. Just visuals + text overlay.
Her morning. Her ritual. Her products. Her softness.
ASMR-adjacent — slow, intentional, beautiful.

### Format 5 — The Educational Series
5-part series on one topic (skincare ingredients, hair health, etc.)
Each part ends with a hook to the next.
Drives follows and saves.

---

## Viral Copy Hooks (For Captions + Ads)

*"She doesn't have a morning routine. She has a ritual."*
*"Most brands were built for a customer they imagined. We built ours for the real one."*
*"Softness isn't passive. It's the most disciplined thing a woman can choose."*
*"The right products don't complete you. They honor what's already there."*
*"Beauty should give back what the day takes. That's the whole philosophy."*

---

## Viral Hook Testing Protocol

For every 5 pieces of content — test at least 2 different hooks on the same content.

**Track:**
- Views in first hour (TikTok signal)
- Watch time / completion rate
- Shares and saves
- Comments (are they engaging with the topic?)

**Build a swipe file** of top-performing hooks in `13_Research_And_Inspiration/Swipe_Files.md`

# 🎁 MVQUEEN — Giveaway Systems

---

## Purpose

Run giveaways that build genuine community, grow a qualified audience, and reinforce brand positioning — not giveaways that attract freebie-seekers who disappear after winning.

**MVQUEEN giveaway rule:** Every giveaway should attract the exact customer we want — not just anyone who wants free stuff.

---

## Giveaway Types

| Type | Goal | Prize | Entry Method |
|------|------|-------|-------------|
| Community celebration | Reward existing followers | Hero product or bundle | Like + follow + tag |
| Launch amplification | Drive awareness for new product | New product featured | Follow + share to stories |
| UGC giveaway | Generate authentic content | Product bundle | Post with branded hashtag |
| Email list growth | Build owned audience | High-value bundle | Email signup |
| Milestone celebration | Community appreciation | Luxury bundle | Engagement-based |

---

## Giveaway Architecture

### Pre-Launch (5-7 days before)
```
□ Define goal — what does success look like beyond entries?
□ Select prize — must be desirable enough to share
□ Draft legal copy (no purchase necessary, open to residents of [X], void where prohibited)
□ Create announcement graphics
□ Plan entry mechanic — simple enough to complete, intentional enough to qualify
□ Set end date and winner selection method
□ Prepare winner notification DM template
```

### Launch Day
```
□ Post announcement with clear entry instructions
□ Stories series explaining the giveaway
□ Email segment notification (if applicable)
□ Pin announcement post to profile
```

### During Giveaway (Daily)
```
□ Engage with everyone who enters — like their tags, reply to comments
□ Post reminder stories at day 3 and day 6
□ Share UGC entries to stories (with permission)
□ Monitor for fake accounts or spam entries
```

### Close and Winner Selection
```
□ Use random selection tool (not manual — transparent)
□ Verify winner account is genuine
□ DM winner within 24 hours
□ Announce winner publicly (with their permission)
□ Fulfill prize within 5 business days
```

---

## Entry Mechanics by Goal

**Goal: Follower growth**
Entry: Follow + like post + tag 1 friend
Bonus entry: Share to stories

**Goal: Email list growth**
Entry: Sign up at link in bio
Bonus entry: Tag a friend in comments

**Goal: UGC generation**
Entry: Post a photo using [branded hashtag]
No follow required — lowers barrier, increases content volume

**Goal: Community reward**
Entry: Comment with answer to a question
Creates engagement signal to algorithm

---

## Prize Strategy

**What makes a good MVQUEEN giveaway prize:**
- High perceived value (not just high retail value)
- Reflects brand identity — not just "a bundle of stuff"
- Something she would have bought anyway
- Presented beautifully — even in a photo

**Prize tiers:**
| Tier | Value | Contents |
|------|-------|---------|
| Starter | $50-100 | 2-3 hero products |
| Ritual | $100-200 | Full ritual bundle + packaging |
| Luxury | $200-300 | Full collection + exclusive packaging + card |

---

## Giveaway Copy Template

**Caption formula:**
```
[Opening — atmosphere or brand truth]
[The gift — describe it like you love it]
[Why we're giving it — real reason]
[Entry instructions — numbered, clear, simple]
[End date]
[Legal line]
```

Example opening: *"For the woman who deserves a ritual that's entirely her own — we're giving one away."*

---

## Post-Giveaway Actions

```
□ Thank all participants in stories
□ Analyze follower retention rate (how many unfollow after giveaway?)
□ Segment new followers for nurture sequence
□ Collect UGC entries for repurposing (with permission)
□ Log performance in Campaign Log database
```

**Health check:** If more than 30% of followers gained unfollow within 7 days — the prize attracted the wrong audience. Adjust for next giveaway.

**MVQUEEN seasonal rule:** Every seasonal campaign must connect to a genuine brand truth. Not just "it's Valentine's Day, here's a sale."

## Tier 1 Campaigns (Full Production)

These campaigns receive full creative treatment: email sequence, social series, paid ads, product features, possible limited edition.

**Valentine's Day — "Love Yourself First"**
Theme: Feminine self-love — not performative, not dependent on a partner
Products: Skincare ritual sets, fragrances, anything she'd buy for herself
Copy angle: *"She doesn't wait to be given something luxurious. She chooses it herself."*

**Mother's Day — "The Women Who Taught Us Softness"**
Theme: Tribute to feminine legacy — mothers, mentors, the women who shaped us
Products: Gift sets, luxury bundles, anything giftable with beautiful packaging
Copy angle: *"For the woman who showed you what strength looked like when it was soft."*

**Holiday / December — "Give Her Something That Means Something"**
Theme: Intentional gifting — against the noise of mass consumerism
Products: Full gift sets, luxury bundles, wrapping and presentation elevated
Copy angle: *"Not just a gift. A ritual she'll carry into the new year."*

**New Year — "The Ritual of Becoming"**
Theme: Intentional new beginning — not resolution culture, but ritual-setting
Products: Full ritual kits, skincare systems, anything that signals a fresh start
Copy angle: *"Not new year, new you. New year, more you."*

---

## Tier 2 Campaigns (Lighter Touch)

These campaigns receive: 1-2 emails, social content series, no paid ads unless performing.

- Spring launch: New season, new ritual, fresh arrivals
- Summer: Slow down, simplify, summer skin/hair focus
- Fall: Deeper rituals, richer textures, cozy femininity

# 🤝 MVQUEEN — Affiliate Program

---

## Purpose

Build a performance-based affiliate ecosystem that extends MVQUEEN's reach through aligned creators, customers, and communities — while maintaining brand integrity and generating measurable revenue.

---

## Affiliate Program Architecture

### Program Tiers

| Tier | Who | Commission | Perks |
|------|-----|-----------|-------|
| **Brand Ambassador** | Loyal customers, micro-creators (1K-10K) | 15% | Early access, gifting, personal code |
| **Creator Partner** | Content creators (10K-100K) | 20% | Monthly gifting, dedicated support, performance bonuses |
| **Elite Partner** | Established influencers (100K+) | Custom | Full partnership, co-creation opportunities |

---

## How the Program Works

**Tracking:** Unique affiliate link + discount code per affiliate
**Cookie window:** 30 days (customer has 30 days after clicking to purchase)
**Payout:** Monthly, minimum $50 threshold, via PayPal or bank transfer
**Dashboard:** Affiliates access their own dashboard showing clicks, conversions, earnings

---

## Affiliate Application Process

```
1. Applicant submits application (form on website or via DM)
2. Review against criteria (below) — 48 hour response
3. Approved: Onboarding email with welcome kit, code, link, and brand guidelines
4. Declined: Warm response — invite to reapply after 3 months
```

**Application criteria:**
| Criteria | Minimum Standard |
|----------|----------------|
| Aesthetic alignment | Feminine, soft, elevated — not jarring contrast |
| Audience fit | Primarily women 20-45 |
| Engagement quality | Real engagement, not inflated |
| Content quality | Consistent, intentional, brand-adjacent |
| Values alignment | No content that contradicts MVQUEEN values |

---

## Affiliate Onboarding Kit

Every approved affiliate receives:
```
□ Welcome message — personal, warm
□ Unique discount code (10% off for their audience)
□ Unique tracking link
□ Brand guidelines PDF (condensed — voice, visuals, dos/don'ts)
□ Product knowledge guide
□ Content ideas (suggestions, never scripts)
□ First product shipment — for genuine use and content
□ Dashboard access link
```

---

## Affiliate Content Guidelines

**They should:**
- Share honest experiences with products
- Use their natural voice — not MVQUEEN's voice
- Disclose the partnership (#ad or #affiliate per FTC rules)
- Share their real ritual and how MVQUEEN fits it

**They should not:**
- Make medical or therapeutic claims
- Compare MVQUEEN negatively to competitors
- Use aggressive discount or urgency language
- Post content that contradicts brand values
- Share internal pricing or supplier information

---

## Affiliate Performance Management

| Metric | Review | Action |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Clicks | Monthly | Low clicks = content support needed |
| Conversion rate | Monthly | Low CVR = audience mismatch or code issue |
| Revenue generated | Monthly | Top performers → upgrade tier |
| Content quality | Quarterly | Off-brand content → coaching or exit |
| Activity level | Quarterly | Inactive 90 days → check in or offboard |

**Top affiliate rewards:**
- Bonus commission on milestone months
- Exclusive product access before launch
- Feature on MVQUEEN's main channels
- Co-creation invitation for high performers

# 📱 MVQUEEN — Viral Content Systems

---

## Purpose

Build repeatable content systems that consistently produce shareable, saveable, and viral content — engineered from emotional truth and brand intelligence, not luck.

---

## The MVQUEEN Virality Formula

```
EMOTIONAL TRUTH + PRECISE LANGUAGE + RIGHT FORMAT + TIMING = SHAREABILITY
```

Viral content at MVQUEEN hits one of these triggers:
- She recognizes herself immediately
- She learns something she wishes she knew sooner
- She feels something she wants others to feel
- She sees something so beautiful she needs to share it

---

## Evergreen Viral Content Categories

### Category 1 — Identity Content
Content that makes her feel deeply seen.

**Formats:** Carousel, caption, short video
**Examples:**
- "Signs you're a woman who chooses softness intentionally"
- "The difference between a routine and a ritual"
- "For the woman who is done performing and ready to just live"

**Why it spreads:** She tags her friends who are "exactly like this."

---

### Category 2 — Revelation Content
Content that reframes something she thought she understood.

**Formats:** TikTok video, carousel, long caption
**Examples:**
- "Why your skincare isn't working (and it's not the products)"
- "The real reason luxury feels intimidating — and why it shouldn't"
- "What 'soft life' actually means vs how it's been sold to you"

**Why it spreads:** She saves it and shares it with "you need to see this."

---

### Category 3 — Aesthetic Content
Content so visually beautiful it demands to be shared.

**Formats:** Reels, static posts, Pinterest pins
**Elements:** Soft lighting, intentional styling, brand color palette, unhurried energy
**Why it spreads:** Pure visual desire — she wants this feeling.

---

### Category 4 — Educational Series
Multi-part content that drives follows and return visits.

**Formats:** TikTok series, Instagram carousels, email series
**Examples:**
- "The MVQUEEN Skin Series" — 5 parts
- "Building your ritual from scratch" — 7 parts
- "Understanding your skin type" — 4 parts

**Why it spreads:** Saves and follows — she wants the whole series.

---

### Category 5 — Trend Participation (Filtered)
Participating in trends that align with brand identity.

**Filter test:** Does this trend align with softness, femininity, intentional living, or luxury?
If yes → participate with MVQUEEN angle.
If no → skip. Never force brand identity into a misaligned trend.

---

## Carousel System

**MVQUEEN carousel formula:**

| Slide | Content |
|-------|---------|
| 1 (Cover) | Hook — makes her stop and swipe |
| 2-7 (Body) | Value delivery — one point per slide, simple and clear |
| Last slide | CTA + brand moment |

**Cover slide rules:**
- Text readable at thumbnail size
- Curiosity gap or strong statement
- Brand colors — visually consistent

**Body slide rules:**
- One idea per slide
- Short text — never walls of copy
- Visual consistency throughout
- Slide 7 is your strongest point — save the best for almost-last

**Types:**
- Educational listicle: "5 signs your beauty routine needs to become a ritual"
- Product showcase: "Your MVQUEEN ritual, built"
- Myth-busting: "Luxury skincare myths — debunked"
- Before/after (emotional): "Before MVQUEEN / After MVQUEEN (the feeling)"

---

## Viral Content Calendar Integration

Distribute viral content types across the week:

| Day | Content Type | Platform Priority |
|-----|-------------|-----------------|
| Monday | Identity content | Instagram + TikTok |
| Tuesday | Educational | TikTok + Carousel |
| Wednesday | Aesthetic | Instagram Reel + Pinterest |
| Thursday | Revelation / hot take | TikTok |
| Friday | Community / UGC | Instagram |
| Saturday | Aesthetic lifestyle | Instagram |
| Sunday | Softer, personal | Stories + Instagram |

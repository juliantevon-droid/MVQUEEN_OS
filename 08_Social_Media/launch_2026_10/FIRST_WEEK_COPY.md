# First week — copy and capture sheet

**Draft content; nothing in this file has been scheduled or published.** Use [the profile kit](PROFILE_KIT.md) for account routing and [the calendar](CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv) for source-specific destination links. Day numbers begin when the verified profiles and assets are ready, not on a preselected date.

MVQUEEN and Miss.Princess have separate profiles under the current brand policy. Use each script only on the named brand's channels. The same clean master can be adapted across that brand's Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube accounts. Caption and subtitle every spoken video. Use owned footage and original voice/audio, or music explicitly licensed for the business account and destination platform; a song available on one platform is not automatically reusable elsewhere.

Before capture, open the current public brand page in a logged-out browser. Keep customer details, order screens, account sessions, and payment information out of recordings. Confirm product names, visuals, and interface labels against what is actually visible. None of the scripts requires a claim about a material, health benefit, popularity, shipping time, or price.

## Capture and posting checklist

- Read each finished caption against the [forbidden words list](../../06_Tone_And_Voice/Forbidden_Words.md); canonical brand names remain correctly capitalized.
- Keep one main action per adaptation: explore, save, or follow. Optional brand hashtags are `#MVQUEEN` or `#MissPrincess` on the matching account; add only accurate topic tags, without promising reach.
- Use current logo files, real page recordings, and licensed media. Label editorial inspiration clearly; avoid suggesting that props are included with an order.
- Preview subtitles, thumbnail crop, image alt text, and contrast on a phone. Correct automatic captions before publishing.
- Confirm the exact account and brand, destination link, and asset before pressing Publish. Save the resulting live URL and timestamp afterward; a draft export is not proof of publication.

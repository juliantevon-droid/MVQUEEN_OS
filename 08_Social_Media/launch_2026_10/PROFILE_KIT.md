## Visual setup

Use the approved, current logo for each sister brand. Do not recreate the logo, alter its lettering, or invent product imagery. Source the final logo from the owner-approved artwork used on the live storefront and confirm the crop at small size.

| Item | Working export | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Avatar | Square, 1080 × 1080 | Center the approved logo with generous clear space; preview the circular crop. |
| Short video | 1080 × 1920, 9:16 | Capture a clean master without another platform's watermark. Keep subtitles away from interface overlays. |
| Feed carousel | 1080 × 1350, 4:5 | One thought per slide; readable contrast; describe meaningful images in alt text. |
| Pinterest still | 1000 × 1500, 2:3 | Clear title and one visual idea; use the matching brand's logo and destination. |
| YouTube banner | 2560 × 1440 working canvas | Keep the logo and short message in the central safe area; verify mobile/desktop previews in the editor. |

Use the current [color system](../../02_Brand_Identity/color_system.md), which supersedes older palette suggestions: light backgrounds, charcoal text, gold accents for MVQUEEN; the established rose/blush direction for Miss.Princess. Decorative gold is not small body text. Do not assume props shown in a lifestyle image are products sold by the store.

## A sustainable first month

Batch three short-video masters and one carousel each week; derive five distinct Pinterest images from that footage and those slides. The [30-day calendar](CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv) contains 12 original videos, four carousels, and 20 pins: **76 individual platform publications** if every adaptation is used. That count excludes optional Stories and replies. It is a working target, not an obligation or an automatic schedule.

Start with the owned accounts that are ready. If time is short, publish the three videos on Instagram and TikTok first, then reuse the strongest on Facebook/YouTube and add the five pins. Preserve brand separation. Set publishing dates only after the business timezone and audience are known; do not invent “best posting times.”

Metricool Free allows one brand and 20 posts actually published through its planner per calendar month in total; drafts do not count, and each destination publication counts separately. The default method here is the platforms' native publishing tools. If using the optional scheduler, check the remaining quota; a video published to four networks consumes four publications. Do not create duplicate free workspaces to evade limits. See [setup and connections](SETUP_AND_CONNECTIONS.md) for the current connection state and official sources.

Record the live URL, publication time, platform reach/views, saves, meaningful comments, profile visits, and website visits where available. Review the first seven days without inventing benchmarks; keep the useful topics and improve unclear openings. Organic work has no ad spend in this plan, but it requires content production and does not guarantee reach or sales.

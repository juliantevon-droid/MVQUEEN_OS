## Free publishing arrangement

Metricool's current Free plan supports **one brand**, **one profile per network**,
**20 published planner posts per calendar month**, and **30 days of analytics**.
Each destination counts separately: one video published to Instagram, Facebook,
TikTok and YouTube uses four posts. Drafts do not consume that publication
allowance. Check the planner's current counter before allocating anything.

Use its single brand for MVQUEEN initially if that slot is available. Manage
Miss.Princess through native platform tools. Native publishing also covers
MVQUEEN posts beyond the allowance. Do not upgrade or displace an existing brand
to fit this plan. The calendar defaults to native/manual publishing and does not
promise automatic scheduling of all entries.

Optional maximum allocation, if the monthly allowance is unused: four MVQUEEN
videos across four destinations (16 publications), plus four Pinterest pins
(4 publications). All remaining posts use native tools. If existing usage is
nonzero, reduce this allocation accordingly.

Free does not include LinkedIn/X connections or Metricool SmartLinks. The
existing store brand pages serve as destinations without a paid link-in-bio
service. No ad account, boost, paid subscription or paid campaign is required.

are the setup reference; completed connections do not need to be recreated.

1. Sign in to an existing Metricool account or create its Free account. Check
   whether the one-brand slot already contains profiles before adding anything.
2. Connect the owned social profiles in Metricool using each platform's sign-in
   and authorization flow. Confirm the brand/account shown on each screen.
3. In ChatGPT on the web, open Apps, select **Metricool**, and complete its
   installation/connection and OAuth sign-in flow.
4. After access is available, read back the brand and network identities. Match
   them to the owner-confirmed public URLs and record connection state.
5. Prepare posts as drafts with correct media, captions and destinations.
   Account setup does not by itself approve publication. Final scheduling needs
   the actual start date, timezone and publishing instruction.

Metricool MCP works on Free via OAuth. Its separate token-based API requires a
higher plan and is not needed here. The MCP can inspect brands, analytics and
planner content; it currently does not expose inbox replies, post comments,
SmartLinks, or advertising campaign management.

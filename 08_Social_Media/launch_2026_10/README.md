# October 2026 organic launch kit

Prepared for MVQUEEN and Miss.Princess. This kit contains drafts and a proposed
workflow. It does not establish that accounts exist or that content is scheduled.

1. [Set up and connect accounts](SETUP_AND_CONNECTIONS.md): current status,
   owner sign-in steps, five core platforms and the free publishing arrangement.
2. [Apply the profile copy](PROFILE_KIT.md): bios, proposed handles and brand
   destinations. Existing owned accounts take precedence.
3. [Produce the first week](FIRST_WEEK_COPY.md): captions, pinned posts and
   shot lists using real, owned or licensed assets.
4. [Use the 30-day calendar](CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv): draft production and
   publishing tasks. Assign real dates only after the account setup and launch
   checks are complete.
5. [Complete the launch checks](../../PRODUCTION_READINESS/LAUNCH_COMPLETION_PLAN_2026-10-14.md):
   scorecard, order verification, performance and measurement.

Keep MVQUEEN and Miss.Princess content on their corresponding accounts. The
existing strategy documents remain the source of brand direction; this kit
provides a smaller first-month workload. Begin with free native tools, and use
Metricool's limited free allowance only after the correct profiles are connected.

Review outcomes weekly: saves, qualified store visits, checkout starts, orders
and useful customer questions. Organic reach costs time and does not guarantee
sales.

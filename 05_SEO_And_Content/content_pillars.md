# Content Pillars
## MVQUEEN_OS / 05_SEO_And_Content

---

## Purpose

Defines the 5 pillar categories that all MVQUEEN content must fall into. Every piece of content answers one of these questions.

---

## Pillar 1: TRANSFORMATION (40% of content)

**Question:** "How do I become who I want to be?"

**Content Types:**
- Transformation stories (before/after journeys)
- Capability building (how-tos, tutorials)
- Identity shifts ("From X to Y in 30 days")
- Confidence building (belief statements)

**Examples:**
- "5 Steps to Your Glow Era"
- "How I Built My Daily Ritual"
- "The Confidence Currency: When You Stop Apologizing"

**Why:** Customers don't want products; they want to become different.

---

## Pillar 2: EDUCATION (25% of content)

**Question:** "What do I need to know?"

**Content Types:**
- Ingredient deep-dives
- Skincare science explained
- Product selection guides
- Trend analysis (why trends matter)

**Examples:**
- "What Hyaluronic Acid Actually Does (And Doesn't)"
- "Why Quality Matters: The Luxury Formula"
- "This Year's Luxury Trends Decoded"

**Why:** Educated customers make confident purchases and stay loyal.

---

## Pillar 3: COMMUNITY (20% of content)

**Question:** "Who's doing this with me?"

**Content Types:**
- Customer spotlights
- Community stories
- Events and gatherings
- Brand team introductions
- Movement/cause alignment

**Examples:**
- "Meet Michelle: From Struggling to Self-Care Queen"
- "How Our Community Changed Me"
- "Join Us: This Month's Ritual Circle"

**Why:** Belonging drives loyalty and advocacy.

---

## Pillar 4: INSPIRATION (10% of content)

**Question:** "What's possible?"

**Content Types:**
- Mood boards
- Aesthetic inspiration
- Seasonal themes
- Editorial looks
- Behind-the-scenes

**Examples:**
- "Summer Glow Mood Inspo"
- "Behind the Scenes: How We Create"
- "Your Glow Moment: 5 Aesthetic Ideas"

**Why:** Inspiration drives engagement and imagination.

---

## Pillar 5: TIMELY (5% of content)

**Question:** "What's happening now?"

**Content Types:**
- Product launches
- Limited drops
- Seasonal guides
- Trending topics (with MVQUEEN angle)
- Special moments

**Examples:**
- "New: The Velvet Authority Lip Lands Today"
- "Holiday Gift Guide: For Every Glow Era"
- "This Week's Luxury Trend We're Obsessed With"

**Why:** Timeliness keeps brand fresh and relevant.

---

## Content Ratio

**In a month of content:**
- Transformation: 8-10 posts
- Education: 5-6 posts
- Community: 4-5 posts
- Inspiration: 2-3 posts
- Timely: 1-2 posts

**Total:** ~20 posts/month = ~5 per week

---

## Content Approval Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- [ ] Falls into one of the 5 pillars
- [ ] Aligns with MVQUEEN voice
- [ ] Delivers value (not just promotional)
- [ ] Uses on-brand imagery/design
- [ ] Has clear CTA (not salesy)
- [ ] Meets accessibility standards
- [ ] Grammatically flawless
- [ ] Fact-checked (if claiming benefits/results)

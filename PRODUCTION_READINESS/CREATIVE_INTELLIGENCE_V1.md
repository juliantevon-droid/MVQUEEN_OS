# MVQUEEN Creative Intelligence V1

## Purpose

Turn a production-ready product into structured creative briefs for paid and organic channels without inventing product facts, proof, or performance claims.

## Creative system

Each product may generate channel-specific briefs across:

- Meta static/carousel
- Meta short-form video
- TikTok short-form video
- Google search/product messaging
- UGC concept
- Instagram organic
- Email
- SMS

Creative output is a **brief**, not automatic ad publication.

## Required creative dimensions

Every brief should define:

1. audience/context
2. customer desire or problem
3. creative angle
4. hook direction
5. product-specific evidence available
6. visual direction
7. primary message
8. CTA
9. claim constraints
10. funnel stage
11. testing variable

## Testing framework

Creative testing should isolate meaningful variables instead of changing everything simultaneously.

Recommended variables:

- hook
- angle
- opening visual
- proof format
- offer presentation
- CTA
- audience/context

A creative record should identify the variable being tested so performance can be attributed to a deliberate hypothesis.

## Claim governance

Creative may use verified facts and approved brand positioning. It must not create:

- medical or therapeutic claims
- fabricated reviews or testimonials
- fabricated statistics
- invented certifications
- unsupported superiority claims
- guaranteed outcomes
- false scarcity
- false discounts

UGC concepts must be clearly marked as concepts unless actual customer content exists.

## Channel principles

**Meta:** identity + desire + visual interruption + concise product value.

**TikTok:** native-feeling hook + demonstration/context + product relevance + CTA.

**Google:** search intent + accurate product/category language + approved value proposition.

**UGC:** believable customer-context scenario without pretending to be a real customer.

**Email:** subject/opening + product story + proof + CTA.

**SMS:** concise value + approved offer/context + CTA.

## Creative lifecycle

`BRIEF → QA → APPROVAL → TEST → MEASURE → LEARN → ITERATE`

No creative should be treated as a proven winner before measurement confirms performance.

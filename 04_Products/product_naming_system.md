# Product Naming System
## MVQUEEN_OS / 04_Products

---

## Purpose

Product names are brand architecture. They must evoke emotion, signal luxury, and be memorable.

---

## Naming Formula

### Core Formula: [Descriptor] + [Benefit/Experience] + [Category]

**Examples:**
- "Luminous Ritual Cream" — [Glow] + [Ritual] + [Moisturizer]
- "Velvet Authority Lip" — [Texture] + [Confidence] + [Lipstick]
- "Signature Soft Oil" — [Identity] + [Texture] + [Product Type]

---

## Descriptor Categories

### Sensory (Texture/Feeling)
- Velvet, Silk, Cashmere, Cloud, Feather, Butter
- Luminous, Radiant, Dewy, Glossy, Matte

### Emotional (Feeling/Intent)
- Authority, Ritual, Signature, Sacred, Essential
- Confidence, Grace, Power, Ease

### Natural/Ingredient-Based
- Rose, Amber, Orchid, Vanilla, Sandalwood
- Gold, Pearl, Crystal, Silk

### Positioning
- Essential, Core, Hero, Icon, Legacy
- Edition, Collection, Moment, Era

---

## Naming by Category

### Skincare
**Formula:** [Texture] + [Benefit] + Type
- "Silk Repair Serum"
- "Cloud Hydration Mask"
- "Luminous Renewal Oil"

### Makeup
**Formula:** [Finish] + [Vibe/Benefit] + Type
- "Velvet Authority Lip"
- "Radiant Icon Highlighter"
- "Soft Power Eyeliner"

### Fragrance
**Formula:** [Mood] + [Signature Element]
- "Amber Authority"
- "Rose Ritual"
- "Sandalwood Sacred"

### Fashion
**Formula:** [Descriptor] + Silhouette or Type
- "Velvet Authority Blazer"
- "Cloud Wrap Cardigan"
- "Silk Essential Dress"

### Lifestyle/Ritual
**Formula:** [Experience] + Category
- "Ritual Moment Candle"
- "Sacred Bathing Ritual"
- "Signature Care Kit"

---

## Forbidden Naming Patterns

❌ **Generic:**
- "Moisturizer #3"
- "Best Seller Cream"
- "New Serum"

❌ **Overly Cute:**
- "Glowy Glitter Glow"
- "Sweetie Softness"
- "Adorable Lip Gloss"

❌ **Cheap-Sounding:**
- "Value Moisturizer"
- "Budget Friendly Oil"
- "Bargain Beauty Cream"

❌ **Unclear/Confusing:**
- "Thing 5"
- "New Mystery Item"
- "Product X"

---

## Testing Names

Before launch, test each name against:

1. **Luxury Test** — Does it sound premium?
2. **Clarity Test** — Does customer understand what it is?
3. **Memory Test** — Is it memorable (not generic)?
4. **Alignment Test** — Does it fit MVQUEEN brand voice?
5. **Emotion Test** — Does it evoke feeling or benefit?

**Pass minimum 4/5 to approve.**

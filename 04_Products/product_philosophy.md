# Product Philosophy
## MVQUEEN_OS / 04_Products

---

## Purpose

Defines what MVQUEEN products are, what they represent, and the standards they must meet.

---

## Product Doctrine

**Every MVQUEEN product is:**

1. **Intentional** — Created for a specific need, not random SKU
2. **Luxurious** — Feels premium in hand, quality is undeniable
3. **Transformative** — Delivers visible/felt results
4. **Inclusive** — Works across skin tones, body types, abilities
5. **Honest** — Does exactly what we say, no overpromising
6. **Beautiful** — Designed to be displayed, not hidden

---

## Product Categories

### BEAUTY (40% of catalog)
- Skincare (serums, moisturizers, treatments)
- Makeup (complexion, eyes, lips, glow)
- Fragrance (signature scents)

**Philosophy:** Science-backed, ingredient-transparent, results-driven

### FASHION (35% of catalog)
- Signature pieces (investment items)
- Essentials (wardrobe anchors)
- Seasonal drops (limited, on-trend)

**Philosophy:** Timeless quality, design-forward, size-inclusive

### LIFESTYLE (25% of catalog)
- Ritual items (candles, body care, self-care)
- Accessories (jewelry, bags, keepsakes)
- Collaborations (limited, meaningful)

**Philosophy:** Elevates everyday moments, tells a story, creates community

---

## Quality Standards

### Tier 1: Core Collection (Always In Stock)
- Bestsellers that define the brand
- Consistent quality and pricing
- 3-5 year product lifespan planned
- Never discontinued without replacement

### Tier 2: Premium Collection (Seasonal/Rotating)
- Higher price point, luxury positioning
- Limited batches (200-1000 units)
- Ingredient innovation or design excellence
- 6-18 month lifespan

### Tier 3: Limited/Collaboration (Launch-Based)
- 100-300 units maximum
- Unique positioning (artist, season, cause)
- 2-4 week availability
- Premium pricing justified by scarcity

---

## Naming Standards

**Never:**
- Generic names ("Basic Moisturizer")
- Overly cute names ("Glowy Glitter Glow")
- Names that sound cheap

**Always:**
- Evoke emotion or benefit
- Sound luxurious and intentional
- Be pronounceable and memorable
- Reflect MVQUEEN brand voice

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Luminous Ritual Cream" (evokes experience)
- ❌ "Cream #3" (generic)
- ✅ "Velvet Authority Lip" (positioning)
- ❌ "Lip Glow Thing" (cheap-sounding)

---

## Pricing Strategy

### Cost Breakdown (per product)
- Materials: 25%
- Production: 15%
- Packaging/Design: 20%
- Operations: 15%
- Profit: 25%

### Price Tiers
- **Entry Luxury:** $25-50 (accessible luxury)
- **Premium:** $50-150 (investment pieces)
- **Elite:** $150+ (collector items, limited editions)

**Rule:** Price is a positioning tool, not a discount tool. Never race to the bottom.

---

## Product Development Checklist

Before any product launches:

- [ ] Aligns with MVQUEEN doctrine and brand values
- [ ] Fills a real customer need (not just trend-chasing)
- [ ] Quality meets luxury standards (not compromised)
- [ ] Naming evokes emotion and luxury
- [ ] Pricing reflects value and positioning
- [ ] Packaging delivers luxury experience
- [ ] Description uses brand voice and transforms customer
- [ ] Ingredients/materials are transparent
- [ ] Results can be documented/proven
- [ ] Fulfills inclusive + accessibility standards
- [ ] Has clear point of difference from competitors
- [ ] Fits into existing collection narrative

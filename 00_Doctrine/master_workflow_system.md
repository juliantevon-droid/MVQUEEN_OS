# Master Workflow System
## MVQUEEN_OS / 00_Doctrine

---

## Purpose

Defines the operational flow for all MVQUEEN work. Every task, from brand decision to product launch, flows through this system.

---

## The 5-Layer Workflow

### Layer 1: DOCTRINE (Source of Truth)
**Location:** 00_Doctrine/  
**Update Frequency:** Quarterly or per major brand evolution  
**Owner:** Brand Guardian (Julian Tevon)  

**Files:**
- master_doctrine.md — Constitutional principles
- brand_constitution.md — Operational values
- feminine_identity_framework.md — How femininity works
- emotional_intelligence_framework.md — How EI drives decisions
- ai_governance.md — AI usage boundaries

**Rule:** Nothing bypasses doctrine. All work validates against doctrine first.

---

### Layer 2: STRATEGY (Planning)
**Location:** 01_Brand_Strategy/  
**Update Frequency:** Quarterly or per campaign  
**Owner:** Brand Lead  

**Files:**
- Brand_Bible.md — Complete brand definition
- Positioning.md — Market positioning
- USP.md — Unique selling proposition
- Slogans.md — Campaign themes

**Rule:** All strategy aligns with doctrine. No exceptions.

---

### Layer 3: IDENTITY (Visual & Voice)
**Location:** 02_Brand_Identity/ + 06_Tone_And_Voice/  
**Update Frequency:** Upon major refresh  
**Owner:** Creative Lead  

**Files:**
- color_system.md, typography_system.md — Visual system
- brand_rules.md — Creative governance
- photography_direction.md, logo_usage_guide.md — Visual standards
- Tone_And_Voice.md, CTA_Library.md — Voice standards

**Rule:** All creative output validates against identity system. Deviations require written approval.

---

### Layer 4: EXECUTION (Operations)
**Location:** 04-16, 20-39/  
**Update Frequency:** Continuous  
**Owner:** Operations Lead  

**Files:**
- Product systems, content calendar, automation, templates, data tracking

**Rule:** All execution serves the doctrine and strategy. Metrics validate alignment.

---

### Layer 5: INTELLIGENCE (Learning)
**Location:** 14_Data_And_Analytics/ + 31_AI_Knowledge_Base/  
**Update Frequency:** Real-time  
**Owner:** Data Lead  

**Files:**
- Analytics framework, KPI dashboard, health reports
- AI learning loops, performance data

**Rule:** Data informs strategy, not the reverse. Doctrine cannot be compromised by metrics.

---

## The Decision Flow

```
1. Question Arises
   ↓
2. Check Doctrine (00_Doctrine/)
   ↓ (Does it align?)
3. Check Strategy (01_Brand_Strategy/)
   ↓ (Is it on-brand?)
4. Check Identity (02_Brand_Identity/ + 06_Tone_And_Voice/)
   ↓ (Does it look/sound right?)
5. Execute (04-39/)
   ↓
6. Measure (14_Data_And_Analytics/)
   ↓
7. Learn & Iterate (31_AI_Knowledge_Base/)
```

**CRITICAL:** If step 2, 3, or 4 fails, STOP. Go back. Do not proceed to execution.

---

## Exception Process

If you need to break a rule:

1. **Document** what you're breaking
2. **Justify** why (must reference doctrine)
3. **Test** with limited scope first
4. **Measure** impact rigorously
5. **Report** results to brand guardian
6. **Update** doctrine or revert

**Never** ignore doctrine and execute anyway.

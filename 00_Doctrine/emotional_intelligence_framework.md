# Emotional Intelligence Framework
## MVQUEEN_OS / 00_Doctrine

---

## Purpose

Emotional intelligence is the core capability that separates MVQUEEN from competitors. It drives every decision, every message, every product.

This framework documents what emotional intelligence means in MVQUEEN context.

---

## The Four Domains

### 1. Self-Awareness

Knowing your emotional state, values, and impact.

**In MVQUEEN voice:**
- "Recognize what you need. Act on it."
- "Your standards are valid."
- "Self-awareness is self-care."

**In products:**
- Meet customers where they are emotionally
- Acknowledge their unspoken needs
- Create moments of recognition ("This is for me")

### 2. Self-Regulation

Managing emotions without suppressing them.

**In MVQUEEN voice:**
- "Softness is a choice, not a default."
- "You decide your energy."
- "Calm intention beats reactive speed."

**In products:**
- Formulas that soothe without sedating
- Rituals that create stability
- Packaging that communicates control

### 3. Empathy

Understanding and honoring others' emotional experience.

**In MVQUEEN voice:**
- "We see you."
- "Your moment matters."
- "Luxury is inclusive by design."

**In products:**
- Size inclusivity as norm (not afterthought)
- Accessibility as feature (not accommodation)
- Diverse representation as authentic

### 4. Social Intelligence

Navigating relationships with awareness and intention.

**In MVQUEEN voice:**
- Community as chosen family
- Authenticity over performance
- Vulnerability as strength

**In community:**
- Spaces where customers feel truly seen
- Conversations that matter
- Belonging that doesn't require conformity

---

## Emotional Triggers to Honor

### Recognition
"I feel seen and understood"
- Customer: Gets recommendations that match her values
- Packaging: Feels intentional, not generic
- Copy: Speaks directly to her needs

### Empowerment
"I can do hard things"
- Product: Actually works, doesn't overpromise
- Messaging: Assumes capability
- Ritual: Creates momentum

### Belonging
"I'm part of something meaningful"
- Community: Feels real, not manufactured
- Brand: Has values, not just products
- Stories: From people who look like her

### Transformation
"I'm becoming who I want to be"
- Product: Delivers real results
- Messaging: Speaks to future self
- Experience: Creates marker moments

---

## Emotional Mistakes to Avoid

- **Manipulation** — Using emotions to bypass reason
- **Tokenism** — Showing diversity without meaning
- **Gaslighting** — Suggesting customer's needs aren't real
- **Shame marketing** — Creating anxiety to drive sales
- **Extraction** — Taking customer resources without giving back
